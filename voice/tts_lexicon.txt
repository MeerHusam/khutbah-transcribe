# tts_lexicon.txt — How the English voice (tts.js with ElevenLabs eleven_flash_v2) says Arabic words.
# Without it the voice reads them by English spelling rules: "Allah" as "AL-uh",
# "Muhammad" as "muh-HAM-id".
#
# One word per line: the word, then its sounds. Case does not matter. "Allah's" is found
# from "Allah", and "al-X" from X (with the al- joined to a sun letter: al-Tirmidhi is
# said at-Tirmidhi). Words not listed are read the English way.
#
# Sounds are written the way the voice's phonemizer (espeak) writes them, with ˈ just before
# the stressed vowel. Arabic sounds the voice has: q  χ (kh)  ɣ (gh)  θ (th)  ð (dh)  ʃ (sh)
# dʒ (j)  ɾ (r)  ː (long vowel). It has no ʿayn or ḥ: ʿayn is dropped, or ʔ between vowels;
# ḥ is h. Vowels: ɑ (a), ɑː (ā), ɪ (i), iː (ī), ʊ (u), uː (ū).
# Checked 30 Sep 2026: Whisper hears "ɑllˈɑːh" as الله; the default "ˈælə" as "Alan".
