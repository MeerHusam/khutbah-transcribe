DETAILED SUMMARY
================
This Khutbah of Arafah opens by praising Allah who made Hajj obligatory and calls all people to taqwa, Tawhid, and sincere worship of Allah alone as the greatest preparation for the Hereafter. The khatib outlines the pillars of Islam and urges patience, gratitude, and adherence to the divine cosmic laws Allah has set in creation, warning against wrongdoing and reminding the faithful that Allah defends the believers and destroys oppressive nations. He traces the Hajj rites back to the command given to Ibrahim, peace be upon him, emphasizing that pilgrims must keep the Sacred House pure from all political slogans, partisan calls, and divisive behavior, and instead embody brotherhood, excellence in conduct, and truthfulness. The khatib walks through the full sequence of Hajj rites following the Sunnah of the Prophet, peace be upon him, stressing the importance of calm, orderly movement and compliance with the organizing authorities for the safety of all pilgrims. The khutbah concludes with a supplication for the pilgrims, for the unity and well-being of the Muslim Ummah, and for the leaders of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia in their service of the Two Holy Mosques.
