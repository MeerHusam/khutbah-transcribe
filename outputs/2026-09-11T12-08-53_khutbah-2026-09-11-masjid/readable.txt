This Friday khutbah reminds us that water is a great blessing from Allah and that wasting it, even at a flowing river, is forbidden in Islam.

DETAILED SUMMARY
================
This Friday khutbah focuses on water as one of Allah's greatest blessings, citing multiple Quranic verses that highlight its role in sustaining all life. The khatib emphasizes that true gratitude is not merely verbal but is expressed through conservation and responsible use, and he references the Prophet's practice of using minimal water for Wudu and bathing as a model for all Muslims. He draws attention to a specific modern habit of opening water bottles at gatherings and leaving them half-full, warning that a person will be accountable before Allah for such waste. The khatib stresses that water conservation is a collective trust, applying the Hadith that every person is a shepherd responsible for those under his care, and calls on everyone to begin with themselves, their homes, and their mosques. The second part of the khutbah closes with extensive supplications for the country's security, for the leadership of Saudi Arabia, for the armed forces, and for the well-being of all Muslims.

FULL TRANSLATION
================
All praise is due to Allah, the Bestower, the Giver of gifts, who sends down water from the clouds and makes it a source of life and mercy for the lands and the people. We praise Him, glorified be He, and thank Him for His abundant blessings and great gifts. And I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, alone with no partner, and I bear witness that our master, our Prophet, and our leader Muhammad is His servant and His Messenger. May Allah send His peace, blessings, and salutations upon him, upon his family, and upon all his companions. As for what follows, O servants of Allah, I counsel you and myself to fear Allah, for it is the counsel of Allah to those who came before you and to you as well. May Allah make me and you among His righteous servants. O brothers in faith, Allah has blessed us with countless blessings that cannot be enumerated. He, the Exalted and Majestic, commanded that we be grateful for them, promised increase to those who give thanks, and warned those who deny and are ungrateful of punishment. Our topic today, may Allah have mercy on me and on you, is one of those great blessings, a blessing one can never do without, for there is no life for human beings, animals, or plants without it. Allah the Exalted and Majestic has said: O brothers in faith, water is one of the signs of Allah, the Almighty and Majestic, and a blessing that Allah has bestowed upon His servants. Water that He, the Most High, sends down from the sky, water that He brings forth from the earth, and water that He causes to flow through it. There are salty waters in the seas and fresh waters, and in each of them are profound wisdoms and tremendous benefits. And He, the Exalted and Majestic, said: And He, the Exalted and Majestic, said: And We caused to grow therein grain, and grapes, and green fodder, and olive trees, and palm trees, and dense gardens, and fruit, and grass, as provision for you and for your livestock. O servants of Allah, always remember this great blessing, and let us praise Allah, the Exalted and Majestic, and thank Him for making it easy and abundant. Let us reflect on our own situation compared to those who can only obtain water with great hardship, and then only in small quantities that may not even be fit for drinking. We thank Allah the Most High for these blessings. Remind one another to be grateful for them, and raise your children and those under your care to cherish and preserve them through gratitude. For gratitude for blessings is a cause for their preservation and increase. O brothers in faith, water is not something trivial to be wasted, nor a cheap commodity to be squandered. Rather, it is a blessing to be thanked for, a trust to be preserved, and a wealth to be protected. O Muslims, gratitude for blessings is not merely a word spoken by the tongue. Rather, it is preserving the blessing from being lost and making good use of what one has been given in ways that are beneficial. As He, the Exalted and Majestic, said: "If you are grateful, I will surely increase you." Our great Shariah has brought a magnificent methodology that regulates all consumption in all its aspects, with few but comprehensive words. Allah, the Exalted and Majestic, has established this as a guiding principle: So there is no excess in food, nor in drink, nor in clothing, nor in wealth, nor in water. Moderation is guidance and extravagance is deviation. Good management is gratitude and wastefulness is ingratitude for blessings and destruction. And it was part of the guidance of the Beloved, may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him, to practice moderation. From his Sunnah regarding water is conservation. Anas, may Allah be pleased with him, reported: The Prophet, may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him, used to perform Wudu with one mudd and bathe with one sa' up to five mudds. This was narrated by Bukhari and Muslim. And the Prophet, may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him, passed by Sa'd, may Allah be pleased with him, while he was performing Wudu, and said: "What is this extravagance, O Sa'd?" Sa'd said: "Is there extravagance in Wudu?" He said: "Yes, even if you are at a flowing river." This was narrated by Imam Ibn Majah and Ahmad, and graded hasan by al-Albani. So if extravagance is forbidden even at a flowing river, what then of the one who turns on the tap and leaves the water running without need, or chats with someone nearby? And what of the one who uses a great deal when a little would have sufficed? O servants of Allah, if conserving water is an obligation in our homes, then caring for it in the facilities of mosques and public utilities is an even greater trust. So preserve, may Allah have mercy on you, these blessings and this great blessing of water, especially what we find, and all praise is due to Allah, in the abundance of cold and fresh water. We have now reached a point where we can choose from different water sources what we drink and what we buy. All praise, outwardly and inwardly, belongs to Allah. And do not let O servants of Allah, the abundance of water be a reason to waste it, nor let the ease of accessing it be a reason to forget its value. For blessings endure through gratitude, are preserved through good care, are lost through extravagance, and are taken away through ingratitude. O Allah, perpetuate Your blessings upon us. O Allah, bless us in what You have provided for us. O Allah, help us to thank You and to take good care of these blessings. O Allah, protect us from extravagance, wastefulness, heedlessness, and falling short. I say what you have heard and I seek forgiveness from Allah, the Most Great and Majestic, for myself and for you, for our parents, for our spouses, for our children, for our neighbors, for our relatives, and for all Muslims. So seek His forgiveness, for He is the Most Forgiving, the Most Merciful. All praise is due to Allah for His goodness, and thanks be to Him for His grace and favor. And I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, alone with no partner, in reverence and veneration of His majesty. And I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and His Messenger, the one who called to His pleasure. May Allah send His peace, blessings, and salutations upon him, upon his family, his companions, and his brothers, and upon those who followed their path and traced their footsteps. May He include us with them through His grace and generosity, for He is the Most Generous of the generous. As for what follows, My counsel to myself and to you is to fear Allah, the Exalted and Majestic. So fear Allah, that you may receive mercy. O servants of Allah, let us understand that conserving water is not the responsibility of one party and not another, nor of one individual and not another. Rather, it is a trust carried by all, and a responsibility for which each person will be held accountable. The Prophet, may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him, said: "Each of you is a shepherd and each of you is responsible for his flock." This was narrated by Imam Bukhari and Muslim. So let each one of us begin with himself, his home, and those under his care. There is to be no extravagance in Wudu, no waste in bathing, no waste at home, no neglect at the mosque, and no squandering in public facilities. Water is a blessing, so be grateful for it. It is a trust, so preserve it. It is wealth, so do not squander it. And know, may Allah have mercy on me and on you, that conservation does not mean stinginess, and moderation does not mean deprivation. Rather, it means using what is needed and protecting from waste, so that no excess is squandered and no blessing is dissipated. O servants of Allah, let us look at our present situation, especially the blessing Allah has bestowed upon us in this country and others. The blessing of having fresh and palatable water reaching us in our homes, and water available in bottles of all sizes. So let us thank Allah, the Almighty and Majestic, as well as those people of goodness, generosity, and charity who distribute water in mosques and public places. Yet we find that some among us, may Allah guide them and us, take more water than they need. And some of it is left over. We see this at many occasions, gatherings, events, and weddings. You find some people opening a bottle and leaving half of it or more thinking that this somehow reflects sophistication or some other notion. By Allah, such a person will be asked about that water he left behind, since no one else will drink from it after him. It is therefore incumbent upon us, O servants of Allah, to advise one another and to be among the grateful servants of Allah. O Allah, perpetuate for our country its security, faith, prosperity, and stability. Bless us in its waters, its goods, and its provisions. Make us among those who are grateful for Your blessings, who remember Your favors, and who are obedient and repentant to You. On that note, send your peace and blessings, may Allah have mercy on me and on you, upon the one whom we have been commanded to bless. O Allah, send Your blessings upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, as You sent Your blessings upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim. You are indeed Praiseworthy and Glorious. And bestow Your blessings upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, as You bestowed Your blessings upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim. You are indeed Praiseworthy and Glorious. O Allah, be pleased with the Rightly Guided Caliphs, the rightly directed Imams: Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman, and Ali, and with all the companions of Your Prophet, his wives the Mothers of the Believers, and those who followed them in righteousness. And include us with them through Your grace, generosity, and kindness, O Most Generous of the generous. O Allah, O All-Mighty, strengthen Islam and the Muslims. O Allah, O Supporter, grant victory to Islam and the Muslims. O Allah, O Protector, protect Islam, the Muslims, and the lands of the Muslims. O Allah, O Best of Protectors, safeguard our country, the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, its security, prosperity, stability, resources, sacred sites, and those in authority over it, along with its security forces and defenders. And those residing in it and its citizens, O Best of Protectors. O Allah, deal with the aggressing Houthis. O Allah, deal with all the enemies of Your religion. O Allah, deal with all those who intend harm or evil toward our country and all the lands of the Muslims. O Allah, O Lord of the Worlds, turn their plotting into their own destruction and ruin. O All-Powerful, O All-Mighty. O Allah, grant success to our security and defense forces. O Allah, make them see clearly Your enemy and the enemy of our country. O Allah, reward them on our behalf with the best of rewards, O Possessor of Majesty and Honor. O Allah, count their fallen among the martyrs, and heal, heal their sick, O Best of Protectors, O Lord of the Worlds. O Allah, grant success to, protect, and guide our guardian, the Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques, King Salman ibn Abdulaziz, and his trustworthy Crown Prince, Prince Muhammad ibn Salman, and their supporters. O Allah, extend their deeds in Your obedience, and grant them health and well-being. O Allah, strengthen them and provide for them, and let no harm befall them, O Possessor of Majesty and Honor. O Allah, forgive our sheikhs and our scholars. O Allah, forgive our fathers and our mothers and have mercy on them, as they raised us when we were young. O Allah, heal and cure our sick and the sick among the Muslims. O Allah, heal and cure our sick and the sick among the Muslims, and relieve the debt from us and from our Muslim brothers. O Allah, O Lord of the Worlds, O Allah, relieve the debt from us and from our Muslim brothers. And peace be upon the Messengers, and all praise is due to Allah, Lord of the Worlds.
