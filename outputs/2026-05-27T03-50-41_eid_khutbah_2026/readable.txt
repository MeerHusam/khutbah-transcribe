DETAILED SUMMARY
================
This Eid al-Adha khutbah opens with the takbeer and praises of Allah before reminding worshippers to be grateful for reaching this blessed day, which Allah named the Day of the Greater Pilgrimage. The khatib explains the story of Ibrahim and Ismail, peace be upon them, as the origin of the Udhiyah (sacrificial offering), and encourages every financially able Muslim to sacrifice on behalf of themselves and their families, noting that the time for slaughter extends until the sunset of the thirteenth of Dhul-Hijjah. He also highlights the significance of the days of Tashreeq as days of eating, drinking, and remembrance of Allah, and warns against injustice, reminding listeners that wrongs will be settled on the Day of Judgment through the exchange of good and bad deeds. The khutbah then calls on Muslims to be grateful for the blessings of security, stability, and unity, honors the role of women in Islam with glad tidings of Paradise, and closes with supplications for the Ummah, the pilgrims, and the leaders of the land.
