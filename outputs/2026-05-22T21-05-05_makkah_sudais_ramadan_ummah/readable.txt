This Ramadan khutbah calls on Muslims to use the blessed month as a turning point, fasting with purpose, supporting oppressed brothers worldwide, and renewing faith and unity.

DETAILED SUMMARY
================
The khatib opens by praising Allah for the gift of Ramadan and urging the Ummah to treat it as a season of spiritual transformation, multiplied rewards, and renewed commitment to the Book of Allah and the Sunnah. A significant portion of the khutbah is devoted to the suffering of Muslim minorities, particularly in Palestine, Kashmir, and Chechnya, calling on Islamic governments and international bodies to take urgent action and urging Muslims to support their oppressed brothers through aid, du'a, and financial generosity. The khatib then warns against the corruption of the month through immoral satellite channels and entertainment media, calling for firm measures to protect the Ummah's moral fabric, especially the youth. Practical guidance is given on guarding the fast from what diminishes it, increasing Quranic recitation, giving Zakat, upholding modesty, and parents supervising their children. The second khutbah focuses on following the Prophet's صلى الله عليه وسلم example of generosity and worship in Ramadan, urging the congregation to seize the middle ten days of forgiveness before the month passes, and closing with an extended du'a for the Ummah, its leaders, and all the oppressed.

FULL TRANSLATION
================
All praise is due to Allah, who has blessed us with seasons of goodness and distinguished the month of Ramadan with virtue, honour, blessings, and divine gifts, urging us in it to perform acts of obedience and to increase our worship. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, alone with no partner, a testimony that benefits the one who utters it in life and after death. I bear witness that our Prophet Muhammad is the servant of Allah and His Messenger, the finest of all creation, may Allah send His blessings upon him and upon his family and companions, those of virtue and noble honour, and upon those who followed them in goodness for as long as the earth and the heavens endure, and may abundant peace be upon him. To proceed: O Muslim men and women, O fasting brothers and fasting sisters, fear Allah, the Blessed and Exalted, and thank Him for the great occasions He has prepared for you, occasions that deepen faith in the hearts and stir the overflowing emotions in the souls, increasing acts of obedience and narrowing the scope of evil and wrongdoing for individuals and communities alike, and that impart Islamic lessons and faith-inspired effects in unity, solidarity, purity, righteousness, family ties, happiness, cleanliness, goodness, virtue, patience, sacrifice, and dignity. They are like vast and shading trees, sweet watering-holes, and fortified strongholds for the obedient, and irreplaceable opportunities for sinners and the negligent to begin a fresh page in the record of their lives. O assembly of Muslims, among the greatest of these occasions in time, the most momentous in status, and the most profound in impact is what we are living in, the radiant, blessed fragrance of these luminous days and the shining, noble, glittering nights of this blessed month. We draw from its pure spring, we quench our thirst from its flowing stream, we drink from its sweet water, and we inhale its fragrant scent, the month of multiplied good deeds, forgiveness of sins and wrongdoings, elevation of ranks, and removal of stumbles. It is the season of commerce and profit, in which souls journey toward the land of joy. In the authentic hadith, the Messenger of Allah صلى الله عليه وسلم said: 'When Ramadan enters, the gates of Paradise are opened and the gates of Hell are closed, and the devils are chained, and a caller calls out: O seeker of good, come forward! O seeker of evil, hold back! And Allah has those He frees from the Fire, and that is every night.' 'Whoever fasts it and stands in prayer during it out of faith and seeking reward, his previous sins will be forgiven.' This was recorded by the Two Shaykhs from the hadith of Abu Hurayrah رضي الله عنه. Brothers in Islam, O nation of fasting and night prayer, it is truly fitting for the Muslim Ummah, which today lives in one of its darkest eras and one of the most dangerous phases of its life, to make this month a turning point from a life of division and discord to unity and cohesion upon the Book of Allah and the Sunnah of His Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم, and the methodology of the righteous predecessors, may Allah have mercy on them. And let this month be a phase of change for the better in every domain of the lives of individuals, nations, and communities. Through that, the Ummah will restore its ancient glories and its radiant, magnificent civilisation, one that was written by its history, rich with triumphs and victories in this noble month. O nation of Islam, how fervently those who are zealous for their Ummah's condition and those who follow its pains have wished that the blessed month of Ramadan would come upon us while the wounds of the Ummah had healed, its affairs had been set right, its situations had improved, its tragedies had ended, and its eyes had dried of tears. Yet, with deep grief and sorrow, the tragedies of our Ummah continue, its situations remain inflamed, its wounds still bleed, and the blood of its steadfast sons is shed with arrogance and recklessness, amid a global silence, international inaction, and a media blackout. What is the news of those holding firm on the soil of mujahid Palestine? How are the children of the blessed land receiving this noble month, this noble month? What is the news of the First of the Two Qiblahs and the site of the Night Journey of the Master of both worlds? May Allah bring comfort to the eyes and open the hearts through its liberation and hasten its freedom, after the brothers of apes and pigs have done to it what causes foreheads to sweat with shame, while they spread corruption in the land, and Allah does not love the corruptors. What is the news from the soil of Muslim Kashmir, where the spiteful idolaters continue to inflict upon your Muslim brothers there killing and displacement? O nation of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم, the burning front and the most pressing crisis of these days is the issue and tragedy of our brothers in faith in the land of Chechnya. It is now as a wounded body, and its situation is most dire. Indeed, the atheists have dealt with them with defiance, arrogance, tyranny, and persistence, and have plotted a great plot against their faith, their resources, and their land. Our Muslim brothers there are living through Ramadan fasting amid bullet fire, the thick of battles, the sounds of cannons, the bombardment of cities and mosques, the shedding of blood, the killing of innocents, the rape of women, and the orphaning of children. So have mercy, O our Lord, have mercy. The streets have become filled with corpses covered by snow, homes are destroyed, buildings are burning, and the people have no shelter, no food, no water, and no medicine, as if the city of Grozny, which was once considered the jewel of the Caucasus, has today become a ghost city. So peace be upon those steadfast mujahideen. The world has turned a deaf ear to the thunder of shells and the molten rain of bombs and the plunging into the blood of Muslims in one of the most horrific scenes in contemporary history. Which eye can justify withholding its copious tears while it watches the condition of Muslims in this tragic state? And who does not ask about the dire circumstances of thousands of the displaced, the expelled, and the refugees? Would Muslims stand with hands bound in postures of weakness, as if the matter does not concern them? What we fear most is that the curtain will be drawn on this cause, as happened to others like it before, and that the string of our Islamic lands will unravel and their pieces will scatter in the hands of their enemies, with a vital part of the body cut away. So Allah is sufficient for us and He is the best Disposer of affairs; to Allah we complain, and there is no power nor strength except with Allah, the Most High, the Most Great. In the name of the fasting and praying Muslims, we call upon all those concerned, Islamic governments, international bodies, and global organisations, to work tirelessly to stop the horrific massacres and brutal attacks against our Chechen brothers, which represent the most egregious violation of human rights and international norms, especially as these same people are preparing to enter the third millennium while hypocritically calling for human rights. Condemnation and denunciation are not enough; urgent, serious action and firm, decisive measures are indispensable. This is to preserve what remains of the existence and identity of our brothers. And may the praiseworthy efforts of the Land of the Two Holy Mosques, may Allah protect it, in supporting the causes of Muslims and alleviating their suffering and attending to their needs be blessed and placed in their scales of good deeds. So be patient, O sons of Chechnya, your steadfastness has shaken the red tyrants in these times. And the same grief applies to the oppressed peoples and Muslim minorities in many parts of the world. But victory belongs to Islam and its people, and Ramadan is the season of victories. The duty of the Ummah is to seize opportunities before they pass. Brothers in faith and belief, contrasting with this dark picture is a sad and opposing image that reflects the state some of those affiliated with Islam have reached, representing a strange distortion of identity and a loss of goal and means, embodied in media channels, satellite networks, and information networks filled with deformities that scratch the majesty and spiritual beauty of this month of faith, a display of disfigured programmes broadcast by foolish, frivolous people in vulgar scenes and objectionable images that turn the spiritual dimensions of this blessed month into a season of triviality and shamelessness, enticing those seeking forbidden pleasure, claiming that it eases the burdens of fasting, and what a wretched claim that is. This shameful excess in broadcasting cheap entertainment to the Ummah requires firm stands that curb its excesses and restrain its harm, lest this moral rot corrupt the souls of our Muslim generations and expose the entire Ummah to civilisational collapse, at a time when it is most in need of holding firm to its constants and principles, in a world surging with challenges and changes, and in an age dominated by materialism and overwhelmed by temptations. The zealous members of the Ummah have called for the necessity of resisting this satellite excess and this media and information challenge, so that it does not become a gateway through which the destructive negative effects on the Ummah's ideals and values seep in. It is not enough for throats to grow hoarse with appeals; rather, these must be reinforced with deterrent measures that restrain those transgressors, and the Ummah must be warned against them, with an appropriate Islamic alternative put in place to protect the moral and ethical fabric of the Ummah. O leaders of Muslims, this is your opportunity in this noble month to return to Allah and to apply the law of Allah on the land of Allah among the servants of Allah, and to throw your full weight before international forums and global conferences to guarantee the rights of Muslims and Islamic peoples. O brothers in Allah, remember, while you enjoy the blessings of fasting and night prayer in a uniquely spiritual atmosphere through which you soar in rare horizons of faith, brothers of yours who have been persecuted for their religion. Share with them their hopes and pains, participate in their joys and sorrows, extend to them a helping hand, and do not forget them in your support and your supplications. And here is a word to wealthy Muslims and those of means and capability: fear Allah regarding the wealth of Allah that He has granted you and made you trustees over. So be generous and do not be miserly, give and do not withhold, for Ramadan is the season of generosity, giving, and spending. Equally, one must not miss the reminder in this context to pay the obligatory Zakat, which some people have been negligent about, may Allah guide them. It is paired with Salah in the Book of Allah عز وجل and is the third pillar of Islam. O Muslim sisters, fasting disciplines one toward goodness, virtue, and modesty, and it leads a woman along the paths of chastity and dignified comportment. What is seen of the manifestations of immodest display, uncovering, and free mixing is nothing but evidence of a lack of understanding of this great act of worship. So fear Allah, O Muslim women, beware of those who steal honour and the pirates of virtue and the callers to immorality. Adorn yourselves with the hijab, chastity, and modesty, and beware of immodest display, uncovering, and free mixing. Know that among the phenomena deserving of remedy, O servants of Allah, is the phenomenon of some fathers and mothers being negligent in following up with their sons and daughters, may Allah guide them, leaving them free to do as they please during this noble month with no oversight or accountability. So fear Allah, O fathers, follow up with your sons and daughters, and all of you fulfil what Allah has made obligatory upon you. For Allah سبحانه has said: 'O you who believe, protect yourselves and your families from a fire' And the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم said: 'Every one of you is a shepherd, and every one of you is responsible for his flock.' This was recorded in the Two Sahihs from the hadith of Ibn Umar رضي الله عنهما. We are appealing to your sense of protectiveness and manhood, your care for your honour and those under your guardianship, and your duty to fulfil your responsibilities, and we hold a good opinion of you, by the permission of Allah. O assembly of those who fast, all of you guard your fast from what nullifies it and what diminishes it, beware of physical and moral invalidators, grasp the true reality of fasting and its secrets, learn its etiquettes and rulings, and fill its nights and days with righteous deeds, by reciting the Quran, remembrance of Allah, glorification, declaring His oneness, seeking forgiveness, supplication, giving charity, and doing good. And make it an opportunity for affection, love, righteousness, maintaining family ties, and forgiveness. Beware of severing ties, enmity, spite, envy, and hatred. Show Allah the good within yourselves. Renew your repentance from your sins and fulfil its conditions, so that Allah may overlook your sins and make you among those shown mercy and freed from the Fire by His grace and generosity. He is the best One to be asked of and the most generous One to be hoped from. guided. May Allah bless me and you through the two revelations, and benefit me and you through the guidance of the Master of both worlds. I say what I have said and I seek forgiveness from Allah for myself and for all of you and for all Muslims from every sin, so seek His forgiveness, for He is the All-Forgiving, the Most Merciful. The Most Merciful. All praise is due to Allah, who has guided us to Islam as a favour and blessing from Him, and made fasting a means of drawing close to Him and a shield, and a path leading to His pleasure and to Paradise. Honour its status and appreciate it as it deserves, and do not consider any deed you do in it too much. Follow the example of your Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم, for he was the most generous of people, and he was at his most generous in Ramadan. He صلى الله عليه وسلم would increase in Ramadan in charity and kindness, recitation of the Quran, Salah, remembrance of Allah, and seclusion in the mosque, and he would dedicate to it forms of worship that he did not dedicate to any other time. The righteous predecessors, may Allah have mercy on them, followed this same path, setting the finest examples in the excellence of fasting, grasping its true reality, and filling its nights with night prayer and righteous deeds. Al-Zuhri, may Allah have mercy on him, said: 'When Ramadan enters, it is simply a month of reciting the Quran and feeding food.' And they would sit in the mosques and say: 'We will guard our fast and not backbite anyone.' Allahu Akbar! So, by Allah, O servants of Allah, increase in righteous deeds in this noble month, especially reciting the Quran in the month of the Quran, particularly by seizing the honour of both time and place. In the hadith, the Messenger of Allah صلى الله عليه وسلم said: 'Whoever reads a single letter from the Book of Allah will receive one good deed, and a good deed is multiplied tenfold. I do not say that Alif Lam Mim is one letter, but rather Alif is a letter, Lam is a letter, and Mim is a letter.' You welcomed it with joy, reverence, glorification of Allah, and humility, and you will soon farewell it with sorrow and tears. For these first ten days are nearly departing, and many people are turning away from obedience to their Lord. Do you know, O servant of Allah, whether you will witness the rest of the month or not complete it? The wise and discerning person is the one who seizes this as an opportunity to take stock of himself, correct his crookedness, and bring himself to obedience to his Lord before death catches him unawares, for nothing will benefit him then except righteous deeds. And remember, may Allah watch over you, that you are now entering the middle ten days of Ramadan, the ten days of forgiveness, as the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم informed us. So take the means of forgiveness through repentance, turning back to Allah, and constant adherence to righteous deeds, 'And indeed I am Perpetually Forgiving toward whoever repents and believes and does righteousness and then continues in guidance', and may Allah make us and you among those for whom, in this noble month, mercy, forgiveness, and freedom from the Fire have been written. He is Most Generous and Kind. Know, O servants of Allah, that among the best and most elevated of your deeds in your ranks, the purest before your King, and the weightiest in your scales is your abundant sending of blessings and peace upon the beloved, the chosen Prophet, the leader of those who fast and pray, the master of the earlier and later generations, and the Messenger of the Lord of the worlds, the finest of those who prayed and fasted, and the best of those who rose at night for Allah in prayer. Your King, the All-Knowing, has commanded you to do so, as He said in noble words: and peace upon him. O Allah, send blessings, peace, and benedictions upon our master and beloved Muhammad ibn 'Abdullah.
