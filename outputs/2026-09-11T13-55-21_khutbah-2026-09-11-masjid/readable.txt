DETAILED SUMMARY
================
This Friday khutbah centers on the great blessing of water, presenting it as one of the most vital gifts from Allah that sustains all life. The khatib draws on multiple Quranic verses to highlight water's role in giving life to the earth and all its creatures, and urges the congregation to reflect on those who struggle to access even small, clean amounts of it. A key theme is that true gratitude is not just spoken but is expressed through action, particularly through conserving water and avoiding all forms of waste and extravagance. The Prophet's practice of using minimal water for Wudu and bathing is cited alongside a Hadith forbidding waste even beside a flowing river, making clear that frugality with water is a firmly established Sunnah. The second part of the khutbah calls on every individual, household, and institution to take personal responsibility for water conservation, warning that even leaving a partially drunk bottle unused is something a person will be accountable for before Allah.

FULL TRANSLATION
================
All praise is due to Allah, the Bestower of blessings, the Ever-Giving, who sends down water from the clouds and makes it a source of life and mercy for lands and people. We praise Him, Glorified be He, and thank Him for His abundant blessings and magnificent gifts. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, alone with no partner, and I bear witness that our master, our Prophet, and our leader Muhammad is His servant and Messenger. May Allah's peace, blessings, and salutations be upon him, his family, and all his companions. To proceed. O servants of Allah, I counsel you and myself to have taqwa of Allah, for it is the counsel of Allah to those who came before and those who come after. May Allah make me and you among His righteous servants. O brothers in faith, Allah has blessed us with countless blessings that cannot be enumerated. He, Mighty and Majestic, has commanded that they be thanked, and He has promised increase to those who are grateful, and warned those who deny and are ungrateful with punishment. Our topic today, may Allah have mercy on me and you, concerns one of those great blessings, a blessing that is inseparable from our needs, and without which there is no life for human beings, animals, or plants. For Allah, Mighty and Majestic, has said: O brothers in faith, water is a sign among the signs of Allah, Mighty and Majestic, and a blessing that Allah has bestowed upon His servants. There is water that Allah sends down from the sky, water that He brings forth from the earth, and water that He causes to flow through it. There are saltwater seas and freshwater sources, and in each of them lie profound wisdom and great benefits. And He, Mighty and Majestic, said: And He, Mighty and Majestic, said: O servants of Allah, always remember this great blessing, and let us praise Allah, Mighty and Majestic, and thank Him for making it readily available and plentiful. Let us reflect on our own situation and on the situation of those who can only obtain water with great hardship, and then only in small amounts that may not even be fit to drink. Let us thank Allah for these blessings, and remind one another to be grateful for them. Raise your children and those under your care to cherish and appreciate these blessings, for gratitude for blessings is the reason they are preserved and increased. O brothers in faith, water is not something trivial to be wasted, nor a cheap commodity to be squandered. Rather, it is a blessing to be thanked, a trust to be safeguarded, and a wealth to be protected. O Muslims, gratitude for blessings is not merely a word spoken by the tongue alone. Rather, it is preserving those blessings from being lost and using them well in what is beneficial. As He, Mighty and Majestic, said: "If you are grateful, I will surely increase you." Our great Sharia has brought a magnificent method that governs all consumption with few yet comprehensive words, for Allah, Mighty and Majestic, has made this a guiding principle: So there is no excess in food, drink, clothing, wealth, or water. Moderation is guidance and extravagance is deviation. Good management is an act of gratitude, and wasteful spending is ingratitude toward the blessing and a form of squandering. The way of the beloved Prophet, may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him, was one of moderation. Among his Sunnah regarding water was frugality. Anas, may Allah be pleased with him, said: The Prophet, may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him, used to perform Wudu with a mudd of water and bathe with a sa' up to five mudds. Narrated by Bukhari and Muslim. The Prophet, may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him, passed by Sa'd, may Allah be pleased with him, while he was performing Wudu, and said: "What is this wasteful use of water, O Sa'd?" Sa'd said: "Is there wastefulness even in Wudu?" He replied: "Yes, even if you are at a flowing river." Narrated by Imam Ibn Majah and Ahmad, and graded hasan by Al-Albani. If extravagance is prohibited even beside a flowing river, then what about a person who opens the tap and lets the water run while they are inattentive or talking to someone nearby? And what about one who uses a large amount when a small amount would have sufficed? O servants of Allah, if conserving water is an obligation in our homes, then caring for it in the facilities of mosques and public amenities is an even greater trust. So preserve, may Allah have mercy on you, these blessings, and this great blessing of water in particular, especially given what we find, all praise be to Allah, in the availability of cold and pure water, to the point where we now choose from various water sources which water to drink and which water to buy. All praise is due to Allah, outwardly and inwardly. And do not let, O servants of Allah, the abundance of water be a reason to waste it, nor its ease of access a reason to forget its value. For blessings endure through gratitude, are preserved through good care, are squandered through extravagance, and are taken away through ingratitude. O Allah, perpetuate Your blessings upon us. O Allah, bless for us what You have provided us. O Allah, help us to be grateful to You and to care well for these blessings. O Allah, keep us away from extravagance, wasteful spending, heedlessness, and neglect. I say what you hear and I seek forgiveness from Allah, the Great, the Majestic, for myself and for you, for our parents, our spouses, our children, our neighbors, our relatives, and for all Muslims. So seek His forgiveness, for He is the Most Forgiving, the Most Merciful. All praise is due to Allah for His kindness, and thanks are to Him for His guidance and His grace. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, alone with no partner, in exaltation and reverence of His majesty, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger, the one who called to His pleasure. May Allah's peace, blessings, and salutations be upon him, his family, his companions, and his brothers, and upon those who followed his path and walked in his footsteps. May He grant us His favor along with them, by His grace and generosity, O Most Generous of the generous. To proceed. My counsel to myself and to you is taqwa of Allah, Mighty and Majestic. So fear Allah, that you may receive His mercy. O servants of Allah, let us know that conserving water is not the responsibility of one party to the exclusion of another, nor of one individual to the exclusion of another. Rather, it is a trust carried by all, and a responsibility for which each person will be asked according to their capacity. The Prophet, may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him, said: "Each of you is a guardian and each of you is responsible for his flock." Narrated by Imam Bukhari and Muslim. So let each of us begin with himself, his home, and those under his care. There should be no excess in Wudu or bathing, no waste in the home, no neglect in the mosque, and no squandering in any facility. Water is a blessing, so be grateful for it. It is a trust, so safeguard it. It is a wealth, so do not let it go to waste. And know, may Allah have mercy on me and you, that conservation does not mean stinginess, and moderation does not mean deprivation. Rather, it means using what is needed and protecting against waste, so that nothing extra is squandered and no blessing is thrown away. O servants of Allah, let us reflect on our situation here, and particularly the blessing Allah has bestowed upon us in this country and others, of having pure and pleasant freshwater delivered to our homes, packaged in bottles of all sizes. So let us be grateful to Allah, Mighty and Majestic. And likewise, let us appreciate what the generous and charitable people do in distributing water in mosques and public places. We find that some among us, may Allah guide them and us, take more water than they need, and some of it is left unfinished. This is something we witness at many events, gatherings, celebrations, and weddings. You find some people opening a bottle and leaving half of it or more, thinking this is a sign of sophistication or the like. But by Allah, such a person will be held accountable for that water he left, since no one else will drink from it after him. So it is incumbent upon us, O servants of Allah, to advise one another and to be among the grateful servants of Allah. O Allah, perpetuate for our country its security, faith, prosperity, and stability. Bless us in its water, its goodness, and its provisions. Make us among those who are grateful for Your blessings, who remember Your favors, who obey You, and who turn to You in repentance. With that, send peace and blessings, may Allah have mercy on me and you, upon the one whom we have been commanded to honor with our prayers and salutations. O Allah, send Your blessings upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, as You sent Your blessings upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim. You are indeed Praiseworthy and Glorious. And bless, O Allah, Muhammad and the family of Muhammad, as You blessed Ibrahim and the family of Ibrahim. You are indeed Praiseworthy and Glorious. O Allah, be pleased with the Rightly Guided Caliphs, the rightly led Imams: Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman, and Ali, and with all the companions of Your Prophet, his wives the Mothers of the Believers, and those who followed them in excellence. Grant us Your favor along with them through Your grace, generosity, and kindness, O Most Generous of the generous. O Allah, O Mighty One, strengthen Islam and the Muslims. O Allah, O Supporter, grant victory to Islam and the Muslims. O Allah, O Protector, protect Islam, the Muslims, and the lands of the Muslims. O Allah, O Best of Protectors, protect our country, the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, in its security, prosperity, stability, goodness, sacred sites, and its leaders, its security and defense forces, and all who reside and are citizens within it, O Best of Protectors. O Allah, deal with the aggressing Houthis. O Allah, deal with all the enemies of Your religion. O Allah, deal with whoever intends harm or evil toward our land and the other lands of the Muslims. O Allah, Lord of all the worlds, turn their scheming into their own destruction and ruin, O Powerful, O Mighty. O Allah, grant success to our security and defense forces. O Allah, make them aware of Your enemy and the enemy of our land. O Allah, reward them on our behalf with the best of rewards, O Possessor of Majesty and Honor. O Allah, count those of them who have died among the martyrs, and heal, O Allah, heal their wounded, O Best of Protectors, O Lord of all the worlds. O Allah, grant success, protection, and guidance to our guardian, the Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques, King Salman bin Abdulaziz, and his trustworthy Crown Prince, Prince Muhammad bin Salman, and their aides. O Allah, extend their deeds in obedience to You, and grant them, O Allah, health and well-being. O Allah, strengthen them, forgive them, and do not let any plot succeed against them, O Possessor of Majesty and Honor. O Allah, forgive our scholars and teachers. O Allah, forgive our fathers and mothers and have mercy on them, O Allah, as they raised us when we were young. O Allah, heal and cure our sick and the sick among the Muslims. O Allah, heal and cure our sick and the sick among the Muslims, and relieve the burden of debt from us and from our Muslim brothers and sisters. O Allah, Lord of all the worlds, O Allah, relieve the burden of debt from us and from our Muslim brothers and sisters.
