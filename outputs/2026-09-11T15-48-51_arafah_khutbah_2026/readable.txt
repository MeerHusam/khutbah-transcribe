The Khutbah of Arafah calls Muslims to hold fast to tawhid, perform the Hajj rites with sincerity, and return home with renewed faith and unity.

DETAILED SUMMARY
================
This Khutbah of Arafah opens with praise of Allah and a call to taqwa, emphasizing that the greatest preparation for the hereafter is tawhid, worshipping Allah alone and avoiding all forms of shirk. The khatib outlines the pillars of Islam and stresses the importance of patience, gratitude, and obedience to Allah's universal laws in creation. A detailed account of the Hajj rites follows, from the standing at Arafah and the combined prayers to the stoning of the Jamarat, the sacrifice, shaving, and the farewell tawaf, all while urging pilgrims to maintain calmness, follow official guidance, and keep Hajj free from political slogans and partisan calls. The khutbah highlights that Hajj is a manifestation of unity, brotherhood, and solidarity among Muslims of all backgrounds and colors. It closes with heartfelt supplications for the pilgrims, for the Muslim Ummah, and for the leadership of Saudi Arabia, asking Allah to accept the rites and return every pilgrim home safe and victorious.
