This Friday khutbah reminds us that security is one of Allah's greatest gifts and calls us to protect and appreciate it.

DETAILED SUMMARY
================
This Friday khutbah centers on the immense blessing of security that Allah grants to people, explaining how it enables worship, learning, work, and family life to flourish. The khatib highlights the service of the armed forces and security personnel who guard the nation's borders and maintain internal order, citing Hadith that praise those who stand guard in the cause of Allah. A significant portion condemns the Houthis for launching missiles and drones toward Makkah al-Mukarramah, comparing their act to that of Abrahah who marched against the Kaaba, and warning of Allah's punishment for those who violate the sanctity of the Two Holy Cities. The second khutbah builds on this by urging Muslims to show gratitude for security through responsible action, including avoiding the reckless spread of unverified news during security incidents. The khutbah closes with praise for Saudi Arabia's leadership in supporting Muslim causes worldwide and with heartfelt supplications for the protection of Islam, the Muslims, and the oppressed in Palestine, the Levant, Yemen, and Sudan.

FULL TRANSLATION
================
All praise is due to Allah, the Sovereign, the Bestower, vast in His grace and benevolence, who made security a tranquility for hearts and a stability for the homeland, and commanded the preservation of lives, the protection of sacred rights and due rights. So we praise Him, Glory be to Him, and we thank Him for His countless blessings and His favors that cannot be fully enumerated. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger, may Allah send His peace and blessings upon him, upon his family, his companions, and those who follow them in goodness, and may He grant us their company through His grace and generosity. He is the Most Generous of the generous. Now to proceed. I counsel you and myself to have taqwa of Allah, Glorified and Exalted, for it is the counsel of Allah to those of old and those who come after. Brothers in faith, among the greatest of Allah's blessings upon His servants is the blessing of security. Through it, souls find rest, hearts find peace, blood and rights are preserved, and the interests of both religion and worldly life are set aright in its shade. In its shade, the one who prays worships his Lord, Glorified and Exalted, the student seeks his knowledge, the worker strives for his livelihood, and families sleep in peace, assured of their lives, their children, their possessions, and their honor. And because of the greatness of this tremendous blessing, Allah, Glorified and Exalted, reminded the Quraysh of it as a favor upon them, and He said, Glorified and Exalted,: so let them worship the Lord of this House, He thus paired the blessing of security with the blessing of food, because people need sustenance to live by and security to find peace in. Our beloved, and your beloved, the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, said: 'Whoever among you wakes up secure in his dwelling, healthy in his body, and has the food of his day, it is as though the whole world has been gathered for him.' Reported by al-Tirmidhi. So reflect, O servants of Allah, may Allah have mercy on me and on you. How many blessings do we grow so accustomed to that we fail to appreciate their true worth. We go out to our work, return to our homes, and gather with our families and relatives, yet we do not truly know the value of security until we remember that its loss disrupts livelihoods, tears families apart, and weighs down hearts with fear and pain. O Muslims, among the means by which security is preserved, by the grace of Allah, Glorified and Exalted, is what the men of our armed forces and defense personnel do, stationed at the frontiers, guarding the borders, defending the land and its people, staying awake while others sleep, and bearing hardship so that people may live safely in their homes. The Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, said: 'Ribat, one day of guarding in the cause of Allah, is better than the world and everything in it.' Reported by al-Bukhari. And he, peace and blessings be upon him, said: 'One day and night of ribat is better than fasting and standing in prayer for an entire month.' Reported by Muslim. Alongside them, the men of our security forces work to preserve internal security, protect lives and property, combat crime, safeguard rights, and restore what has been wrongfully taken to its rightful owners. And the beloved Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, said: Two eyes that the Fire will never touch: an eye that wept out of fear of Allah, and an eye that spent the night on guard in the cause of Allah. Reported by al-Tirmidhi. May Allah reward them with the best of rewards for all that they give and sacrifice. O servants of Allah, among the greatest of sanctities that must be honored is the sanctity of the Two Holy Mosques, Makkah al-Mukarramah and al-Madinah al-Munawwarah. In Makkah is the Sacred House, toward which the faces of Muslims turn in their prayers, and to which pilgrims performing Hajj and Umrah come. Al-Madinah is the home to which the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, migrated, and in it stands his noble mosque, peace and blessings be upon him. Allah, Glorified and Exalted, said concerning His Sacred House: 'and whoever enters it shall be safe.' And the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, said concerning Makkah al-Mukarramah: 'Indeed, this land was made sacred by Allah.' Reported by al-Bukhari. And the beloved Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, said concerning al-Madinah: 'Whoever intends harm against the people of al-Madinah, Allah will cause him to dissolve just as salt dissolves in water.' Reported by Muslim. Honoring the sanctity of the Two Holy Cities does not stop at words alone, but is shown in protecting those who come to them and ensuring the safety of pilgrims performing Hajj, Umrah, and all visitors. Every act of aggression against innocent people, or any threat to the security of the sacred sites and those who seek them, is a violation of this sanctity and an assault on what Allah has commanded to be protected and revered. O Muslims, among the most heinous and reprehensible crimes is what the enemies of Allah and the enemies of the religion, the Houthis, have dared to do in violating the sanctity of Makkah al-Mukarramah and threatening the security of the Sacred Land of Allah, having launched their missiles and drones toward it. His Eminence, the Grand Mufti of the Kingdom, Sheikh Salih al-Fawzan, may Allah preserve and protect him, likened this vile act to the act of Abrahah the Abyssinian, and stated that they have repeated the history of Abrahah in his intent to harm Makkah al-Mukarramah. How immense is this audacity against the sanctities of Allah. They raise the slogans of Islam, then direct their weapons of war toward the Qiblah of the Muslims, the Sacred Land of Allah, and the place to which the hearts of the believers are drawn. What greater contradiction between claim and action could there be than this, and what crime is more abhorrent than terrorizing the innocent and violating the sanctity of the Secure House that Allah has exalted? Allah has immortalized the account of those who intended harm against His House and made their fate known, and He said, Glorified and Exalted, And He, Glorified and Exalted, also said, Makkah al-Mukarramah is the sanctuary of Allah, and the Kaaba is the Qiblah of the Muslims. Whoever intends it with harm has dared to violate a sanctity that Allah, Glorified and Exalted, has made great. The crime of these Houthis in threatening its security is not merely an aggression against a land, but an act of audacity against a sacred place to which the hearts of over a billion Muslims in every corner of the earth are attached. O servants of Allah, let us thank Allah, Glorified and Exalted, for the blessing of security and for what this blessed state has done, through its leaders, princes, and security and defense forces, for all the effort and dedication they pour into preserving the security of these Two Holy Cities, may Allah protect and watch over them, and over their people and residents. And for all that they give and sacrifice by every means available to them in defending these lands. So let us thank Allah, Glorified and Exalted, for the blessing of security, and let us fulfill our responsibility in preserving it. Let none of us be a cause of harm, nor an aid to corruption, nor a channel for rumors that disturb the people. O Allah, grant us lasting security and faith, and protect us from every evil and harm. I say what you hear and I seek forgiveness from Allah for myself and for you, for our parents, our spouses, our children, our brothers, our relatives, our neighbors, and all Muslims. Seek His forgiveness, for He is the All-Forgiving, the Most Merciful. All praise is due to Allah for His goodness, and thanks be to Him for His guidance and His favors. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner, in exaltation and reverence of His majesty. And I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger, may Allah send blessings upon him, upon his family, his companions, his brethren, and those who follow them in goodness, and may He grant us their company through His grace and generosity. He is the Most Generous of the generous. Now to proceed. My counsel to myself and to you is to have taqwa of Allah, Glorified and Exalted. So be mindful of Allah, that you may receive mercy. O servants of Allah, gratitude for the blessing of security does not stop at words, but is shown in actions and conduct, in respecting the rights of people, preserving property, cooperating with the security forces in repelling danger, and reporting to the relevant authorities whatever requires their intervention through their official channels. It is not responsible behavior, O servants of Allah, when a security incident occurs, to publish it, photograph it, or circulate its news before verifying it and before the officially authorized body has made its announcement. The safety of lives and the preservation of security take priority over rushing to photograph, publish, and broadcast news. Let each one of us be an aid to good, a source of reassurance, and a guardian of trust in the position where Allah, Glorified and Exalted, has placed him. Among what deserves to be reminded of, O servants of Allah, is that this country, and all praise and gratitude belong to Allah, is a giving and generous land. Its leaders have stood in support of the causes of Islam and Muslims throughout history, since its founding under its leader King Abd al-Aziz and the fathers before him. So let us praise Allah, Glorified and Exalted, that its constitution is the Quran and the Sunnah, and that the support of Muslim causes in every corner of the east and west of the earth, and their importance as you know, the cause of Palestine, and the support of their brothers in Sudan, Yemen, the Levant, and everywhere, is an important responsibility they carry. They spare no expense, giving freely of everything precious and valuable for that purpose. Likewise, as you know, this land, and all praise and gratitude belong to Allah, has opened its doors to many people from the east and west. You will not find another land in this inhabited world that, as you know, has opened up and bestowed so generously upon its residents, indeed treating them as honored guests, whoever they may be. So let us praise Allah, Glorified and Exalted, for this blessing, and let us support one another in preserving its security and stability, and let us thank Allah, Glorified and Exalted, and supplicate for our leaders and security forces, asking for their success, right guidance, protection, and care. With that, send your prayers and peace, may Allah have mercy on me and on you, upon the one whom Allah has commanded us to honor with prayers and peace, as Allah, Glorified and Exalted, says: And he, peace and blessings be upon him, said: 'Whoever sends one prayer upon me, Allah will send ten prayers upon him in return.' O Allah, send Your prayers upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, as You sent Your prayers upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim. You are indeed Praiseworthy, Glorious. And bless, O Allah, Muhammad and the family of Muhammad, as You blessed Ibrahim and the family of Ibrahim. You are indeed Praiseworthy, Glorious. And be pleased, O Allah, with the Rightly-Guided Caliphs, the rightly guided Imams: Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman, and Ali, and with all the companions of Your Prophet, his wives the Mothers of the Believers, and those who follow them in goodness, and grant us their company through Your grace, generosity, and kindness, O Most Generous of the generous. O Allah, You are the Almighty, so give might to Islam and the Muslims. O Allah, You are the Guardian, so guard Islam, the Muslims, and the lands of the Muslims from every evil and harm, O Lord of all the worlds. O Allah, grant us lasting security and safety, O Lord of all the worlds, and to all the lands of the Muslims. O Allah, grant lasting security and safety to our land and to all the lands of the Muslims. O Allah, be with our brothers the oppressed in Palestine. O Allah, grant peace and safety to the people of the Levant, the people of Yemen, Sudan, and every place, O Lord of all the worlds. O Allah, deal with those who intend harm against Islam and the Muslims. O Allah, You are the Strong, the Almighty, the All-Knowing, the All-Wise. We ask You by this great and blessed day, for You know all things. O Allah, whoever has intended harm against our land, our sacred sites, our leaders, our security forces, our unity, or the terrorizing of the innocent, O Allah, turn his scheming into his own destruction and ruin. O Allah, we place You as a shield against the throats of our enemies, the enemies of Your religion, and the enemies of our land, O All-Knowing, O Almighty. O Allah, deal with the aggressing Houthis, and with the Persians, and with the aggressing and hateful Zionists, O Strong, O Almighty. O Allah, O Possessor of Majesty and Honor, forgive the Muslim men and Muslim women, the believing men and believing women, and grant special mercy to the fathers and mothers among them. O Allah, set right the husbands and wives, the sons and daughters, O Most Merciful of the merciful. O Allah, relieve the distress of the distressed among the Muslims, settle the debts of us and our Muslim brothers, and heal our sick and the sick of the Muslims.
