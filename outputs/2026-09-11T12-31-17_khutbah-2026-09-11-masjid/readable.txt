This Friday khutbah reminds us that water is a great blessing from Allah and calls us to conserve it, avoid all waste, and show gratitude through our actions every day.

DETAILED SUMMARY
================
This Friday khutbah focuses on water as one of Allah's greatest blessings, describing it as a sign of His power and a mercy for all creation. The khatib calls the congregation to reflect on those who struggle to access clean water and to cultivate genuine gratitude, not just in words but through responsible use. Drawing on Quranic verses and the Sunnah of the Prophet, may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him, the khatib highlights that moderation in water use was a consistent practice of the Prophet, who even cautioned against excess when performing wudu beside a flowing river. The second part of the khutbah emphasizes that conserving water is a collective responsibility, reminding worshippers not to let abundance lead to heedlessness, and pointing to the common habit of opening bottled water and leaving most of it unfinished as an act that will be accounted for. The khutbah closes with heartfelt supplications for the land, its leadership, the security forces, the sick, and all Muslims.

FULL TRANSLATION
================
All praise is due to Allah, the Bestower of blessings, the Giver of gifts, who sends down water from the clouds and makes it a source of life and mercy for lands and people. We praise Him, glory be to Him, and we thank Him for His abundant blessings and great gifts. And I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, alone, with no partner. And I bear witness that our master, our Prophet, and our leader Muhammad is His servant and Messenger. May Allah's peace, blessings, and salutations be upon him, his family, and all his companions. To proceed: O servants of Allah, I counsel you and myself to have taqwa of Allah, for it is the counsel of Allah to those of old and those who came after. May Allah make me and you among His righteous servants. Brothers in faith, Allah has blessed us with countless blessings that cannot be enumerated. He, the Almighty, has commanded us to be grateful for them, and He has promised those who are grateful an increase, and He has warned those who deny and are ungrateful with punishment. Our topic today, may Allah have mercy on me and you, is about one of those great blessings. The need for it never ceases, and there is no life for human, animal, or plant without it. For He, the Almighty, has said: Did those who disbelieve not see that Brothers in faith, water is one of the signs of Allah, the Almighty, and a blessing that Allah has bestowed upon His servants. Water that Allah sends down from the sky, water that He brings forth from the earth, and water that He causes to flow through it. Waters in the seas that are salty, and waters that are fresh and sweet. In each of them are profound wisdoms and great benefits. And He, the Almighty, has said: And He, the Almighty, has said: O servants of Allah, always remember this great blessing, and let us praise Allah, the Almighty, and thank Him for its ease and abundance. Let us look at our own situation and the situation of those who obtain water only with great hardship, and then only in small quantities that may not even be fit to drink. Let us thank Allah for these blessings, encourage one another to be grateful for them, and raise our children and those under our care to preserve them through gratitude, for gratitude for blessings is a means of preserving them and increasing them. Brothers in faith, water is not a trivial thing to be wasted, nor a cheap commodity to be squandered. Rather, it is a blessing to be thanked for, a trust to be preserved, and a wealth to be protected. O Muslims, gratitude for blessings is not merely a word spoken by the tongue. It is preserving them from being lost and using them well in what is beneficial. As He, the Almighty, has said: "If you are grateful, I will surely increase you." Our great Sharia has brought a magnificent methodology that governs all consumption with a few comprehensive words, for Allah, the Almighty, has made this a guiding principle: So there is no extravagance in food, nor in drink, nor in clothing, nor in wealth, nor in water. Moderation is the right path, and extravagance is misguidance. Good management is gratitude, and wastefulness is ingratitude and squandering. The guidance of the Beloved, may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him, was one of moderation, and among his Sunnah regarding water was conservation. Anas, may Allah be pleased with him, said: The Prophet, may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him, used to perform wudu with one mudd of water and bathe with one sa' up to five mudds. This was reported by Al-Bukhari and Muslim. And the Prophet, may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him, passed by Sa'd, may Allah be pleased with him, while he was performing wudu, and said: "What is this extravagance, O Sa'd?" He said: "Is there extravagance in wudu?" He replied: "Yes, even if you are on a flowing river." This was reported by Imam Ibn Majah and Ahmad, and declared hasan by Al-Albani. So if extravagance is forbidden even beside a flowing river, what about the one who opens the tap and lets the water run when there is no need, or chats with the person beside him? And what about one who uses a large amount when a small amount would suffice? O servants of Allah, if conserving water is an obligation in our homes, then caring for it in mosque facilities and public amenities is an even greater trust. So preserve, may Allah have mercy on you, these blessings and this great blessing of water, especially what we find, and all praise is due to Allah, in the abundance of cold and fresh water, to the point that we now choose from among different water sources what we drink and what we buy. All praise is due to Allah, inwardly and outwardly. Do not let, O servants of Allah, the abundance of water be a reason to waste it, nor its easy access be a reason to forget its value. For blessings endure through gratitude, are preserved through good care, are squandered through extravagance, and are taken away through ingratitude. O Allah, continue Your blessings upon us, O Allah bless us in what You have provided for us, O Allah help us to be grateful to You and to care well for these blessings, and O Allah keep us away from extravagance, wastefulness, heedlessness, and neglect. I say what you hear and I seek forgiveness from Allah, the Magnificent, the Majestic, for myself and for you, for our parents, our spouses, our children, our neighbors, our relatives, and all Muslims. So seek His forgiveness, for He is the Most Forgiving, the Most Merciful. All praise is due to Allah for His goodness, and thanks are due to Him for His guidance and His favor. And I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, alone, with no partner, in veneration and exaltation of His majesty. And I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger, the one who calls to His pleasure. May Allah's peace, blessings, and salutations be upon him, his family, his companions, and his brothers, and upon all those who followed his path and traced his footsteps. May He grant us their company through His grace and generosity, O Most Generous of the generous. To proceed: My counsel and yours is to have taqwa of Allah, the Almighty. So fear Allah, that you may receive mercy. O servants of Allah, let us know that preserving water is not the responsibility of one party and not another, nor of one individual and not another. Rather, it is a trust carried by all and a responsibility for which each person will be questioned according to his capacity. The Prophet, may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him, said: "Each of you is a guardian and each of you is responsible for his flock." This was reported by Imam Al-Bukhari and Muslim. So let each of us begin with himself, his home, and those under his care. There is no extravagance in wudu or in bathing, no waste in the home, no neglect in the mosque, and no squandering in any facility. Water is a blessing, so be grateful for it; a trust, so preserve it; and a wealth, so do not let it go to waste. And know, may Allah have mercy on me and you, that conservation does not mean miserliness, and moderation does not mean deprivation. Rather, it means using what is needed and preventing waste, so that no excess is squandered and no blessing is frittered away. O servants of Allah, let us reflect on our situation, especially the blessing Allah has granted us in this land and others, of fresh, pleasant-tasting water reaching us in our homes and also packaged in bottles of all sizes. So let us be grateful to Allah, the Almighty. Likewise, what the people of goodness, generosity, and charity do in distributing water in mosques and public places. Yet we find that some among us, may Allah guide them and us, take more water than they need, and some of it remains unfinished. This is what we observe at many occasions, gatherings, events, and weddings, where some people open a bottle and leave half or more of it, thinking perhaps that this signals sophistication or something of that sort. But by Allah, such a person will be questioned about that water he left, since no one else will drink from it after him. So it is incumbent upon us, O servants of Allah, to advise one another and to be among the grateful servants of Allah. O Allah, preserve for our land its security, its faith, its prosperity, and its stability. Bless us in its waters, its goodness, and its provisions. Make us among those who are grateful for Your blessings, mindful of Your favors, obedient to You, and turning to You in repentance. With this, send your peace and blessings, may Allah have mercy on me and you, upon the one whom we have been commanded to bless with our salutations. O Allah, send Your blessings upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, as You sent Your blessings upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim. Indeed, You are the Praiseworthy, the Majestic. And bestow Your blessings upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, as You bestowed Your blessings upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim. Indeed, You are the Praiseworthy, the Majestic. O Allah, be pleased with the Rightly Guided Caliphs, the rightly directed Imams: Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman, and Ali, and with all the companions of Your Prophet, his wives the mothers of the believers, and those who followed them in righteousness. And include us with them through Your grace, generosity, and munificence, O Most Generous of the generous. O Allah, O Almighty, grant honor to Islam and the Muslims. O Allah, O Helper, grant victory to Islam and the Muslims. O Allah, O Guardian, protect Islam, the Muslims, and the lands of the Muslims. O Allah, O Best of Guardians, safeguard our land, the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, its security, its prosperity, its stability, its goodness, its sacred sites, its leaders, its men of security and defense, and those who reside and live within it, O Best of Guardians. O Allah, deal with the aggressing Houthis. O Allah, deal with all the enemies of Your religion. O Allah, deal with all those who intend harm or evil toward our land and the other lands of the Muslims. O Allah, Lord of all worlds, turn their scheming into their own destruction and ruin, O Powerful, O Almighty. O Allah, guide our men of security and defense. O Allah, make them aware of Your enemy and the enemy of our land. O Allah, reward them on our behalf with the best of rewards, O Possessor of Majesty and Honor. O Allah, accept those of them who have died among the martyrs, and heal their sick, O Best of Guardians, O Lord of all worlds. O Allah, guide, protect, and support our guardian, the Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques, King Salman bin Abdulaziz, and his trustworthy Crown Prince, Prince Muhammad bin Salman, and their aides. O Allah, extend their lives in obedience to You, and grant them health and wellbeing. O Allah, strengthen them, forgive them, and do not allow any plot to prevail against them, O Possessor of Majesty and Honor. O Allah, forgive our scholars and our learned ones. O Allah, forgive our fathers and our mothers and have mercy on them, O Allah, as they raised us when we were young. O Allah, heal and cure our sick and the sick among the Muslims. O Allah, heal and cure our sick and the sick among the Muslims, and relieve us and our Muslim brothers of debt. O Allah, Lord of all worlds, O Allah, relieve us and our Muslim brothers of debt. Our Lord, grant us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire. Glory be to your Lord, the Lord of Honor, above what they describe, and peace be upon the Messengers, and all praise is due to Allah, Lord of all worlds.
