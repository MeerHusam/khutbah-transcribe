This Friday khutbah honors the companions of the Prophet and reminds us to love them, guard our tongues about them, and follow their example.

DETAILED SUMMARY
================
This Friday khutbah is devoted entirely to the immense status and virtue of the companions of Rasulullah صلى الله عليه وسلم, emphasizing that Allah Himself praised them in the Quran and the Prophet testified to their excellence in Hadith. The khatib highlights their greatest gift to the Ummah: the faithful transmission of the Sunnah, including the Prophet's Salah, Zakat, Hajj, and all aspects of worship. He stresses that loving them is a sign of faith, that the methodology of Ahlus Sunnah wal Jama'ah requires sound hearts and tongues toward them, and that reviling them is a grave offense that undermines one's creed. The second part reinforces that the companions are the highest example in knowledge, piety, and reason, that the early generations taught their children to love Abu Bakr and Umar as they taught them the Quran, and that Ahlus Sunnah refrains from engaging in the disputes that arose among the companions. The khutbah closes with supplications for the Ummah, for the Land of the Two Holy Mosques, and against the aggression of the Houthi group, which the khatib condemns as a profound misguidance.

FULL TRANSLATION
================
All praise is due to Allah. All praise is due to Allah, who chose for His Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم the best of companions. They believed in him, affirmed his truthfulness, followed him, honored him, and gave their lives and wealth in the path of his religion, carrying the message of Islam to all generations. We praise Him, the Glorified, and thank Him for His immense blessings. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger, may Allah send His peace and blessings upon him, upon his family, his companions, and all those who follow them in goodness until the Day of Judgment. To proceed: I counsel you and myself to have taqwa of Allah, for it is the counsel of Allah to those of old and those who come after. O servants of Allah, speaking about the companions of Rasulullah صلى الله عليه وسلم is not speaking about pages of history that have turned and closed, nor names that are mentioned and then forgotten. Rather, it is speaking about a generation that Allah chose to accompany the best of His creation, His Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم, to carry His religion and convey His message. They accompanied Rasulullah صلى الله عليه وسلم, heard from him directly, supported him, sheltered him, strengthened him, and gave their wealth and their lives in the service of this religion and in following his Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم. And Allah did not leave the declaration of their virtue to the words and opinions of people. Rather, Allah praised them in His Book and bore witness to His pleasure with them, as He said: And our Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم testified to their excellence, saying: 'The best of my Ummah is my generation, then those who follow them, then those who follow them.' Agreed upon. So when Allah has praised them and His Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم has testified to their excellence, what praise could come after the praise of Allah, and what testimony could come after the testimony of His Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم? O Muslims, among the greatest favors the companions have done for us is their transmission of the Sunnah of our Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم to us. Through their narrations we came to know his Salah, his Zakat, his Hajj, his fasting, his worship, and his guidance. They carried the religion to those who came after them, fulfilled the trust, and conveyed the message. They were faithful to what they heard and truthful in what they transmitted. May Allah be pleased with them and make them pleased. And among what these noble ones are owed by us, O servants of Allah, is that we love them, invoke Allah's pleasure upon them, acknowledge their virtue and standing, and guard our tongues regarding them. For Allah has taught us the supplication of the believers for those who preceded them in faith, saying: Compassionate, Merciful. What a magnificent Ayah it is, nurturing both the heart and the tongue: a prayer for their forgiveness, an acknowledgment of their brotherhood, and a request to Allah to purify the heart of any rancor toward them. And the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم made love of the Ansar a sign of faith. And hatred of them a sign of hypocrisy, as he صلى الله عليه وسلم said: 'The sign of faith is love of the Ansar, and the sign of hypocrisy is hatred of the Ansar.' Agreed upon. So, dear beloved ones, it is for this reason that the methodology of Ahlus Sunnah wal Jama'ah, by the grace of Allah, is characterized by the soundness of their hearts and tongues toward the companions of Rasulullah صلى الله عليه وسلم. They do not make the companions a target for abuse, nor an arena for slander, and they do not spread doubts and lies about them. And the beloved Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم said: 'Do not revile my companions, for if one of you were to spend gold equal to the mountain of Uhud, it would not reach a mudd of one of theirs, nor even half of it.' Agreed upon. What profound words that teach generations their true worth. For the companions combined the honor of companionship with tremendous sacrifice and selflessness, despite the scarcity of provisions and equipment that many of them had, and despite the harm and persecution they endured. Yet through all of that, faith and steadfastness became deeply rooted in their hearts, and resolve and striving grew great within their souls. And they pressed on upon the truth, gave victory to the religion, carried the message, and surpassed those who came after them in a way that cannot be matched. May Allah be pleased with them and make them pleased. It is for this reason that the scholars of the Sunnah spoke harshly about anyone who dares to speak ill of them. Abu Nu'aym, may Allah have mercy on him, said: 'No one stretches his tongue against them except one whose ill intent is directed at the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم, his companions, Islam, and the Muslims.' So let our speech about them, O servants of Allah, always be accompanied by love and reverence, and let our mention of them be connected to invoking Allah's pleasure upon them and to honoring their standing. Let our children learn their biographies, their noble deeds, and their virtues, for they are those who accompanied the best of creation and carried this religion to all of humanity. O Allah, be pleased with all the companions of Your Prophet and reward them with the best of rewards on behalf of Islam and the Muslims. Gather us in the company of our Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم and his companions, not among the disgraced or the misguided. I say what you hear and I seek forgiveness from Allah for myself and for you, for our parents, our spouses, our children, and for all Muslims. So seek His forgiveness, for He is the Most Righteous, the Most Merciful. All praise is due to Allah, who made the stars a protection for the sky, who made His Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم a light dispelling all darkness, and who chose his companions, making them a selected few from among the righteous and a firm barrier against innovations and desires. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger, the seal of the Prophets, may Allah send His peace and blessings upon him, upon his noble and loyal family, and upon his radiant and honorable companions, with abundant salutations until the Day of Judgment and the Final Decree. To proceed: Fear Allah, O servants of Allah, and know, may Allah have mercy on me and you, that the most complete of people in reason, the broadest in knowledge, and the finest in deeds are the companions of Rasulullah صلى الله عليه وسلم. And they are, as Imam al-Shafi'i, may Allah have mercy on him, said: 'They are above us in every field of knowledge, jurisprudence, piety, and reason, and their opinions are a light and a guiding illumination for us.' They were a great generation and a unique era. There has never been and never will be anyone like them. Mentioning their virtues is an act of worship, following their example is guidance and safety, and loving them is a means of drawing closer to Allah and an act of devotion. They spent their wealth and gave their lives in support of the religion of Allah and for the sake of His Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم. And Ahlus Sunnah wal Jama'ah, O servants of Allah, as you know, refrain from engaging in the disputes that arose among the companions of Rasulullah صلى الله عليه وسلم. Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz was asked about the fighting that took place among the companions, may Allah be pleased with them, and he said: 'Those are bloods that Allah has kept my hands free from, and I would not wish to stain my tongue in them either.' O Muslims, the righteous predecessors of this Ummah used to plant in the hearts of their children love for the companions, may Allah be pleased with them. The early generations would teach their children love of Abu Bakr and Umar, may Allah be pleased with them both, just as they would teach them a Surah of the Quran. And reviling the companions is a serious matter and a grave offense that undermines one's creed and indicates ill intent. Imam Ahmad, may Allah have mercy on him, was asked about someone who insults Abu Bakr, Umar, and Aisha, may Allah be pleased with them, and he said: 'I do not consider him to be upon Islam.' O Allah, be pleased with the righteous companions and with the pure household of Your Prophet, O Lord of Majesty and Honor. O servants of Allah, we praise Allah for the blessing He has bestowed upon us of soundness in creed and of following the way of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم without any innovation. A moderate way free of extremism, negligence, and moral laxity. O Allah, O Lord of Majesty and Honor, grant us moderation and balance, and the methodology upon which the companions of Your Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم walked, following him without being extreme or fanatical, O Lord of Majesty and Honor. O servants of Allah, as you know and have witnessed, the ongoing aggression of the Houthi group, who are transgressors and astray in their creed. Be aware, O servants of Allah, that the most recent of their acts was the aggression against the city of Rasulullah صلى الله عليه وسلم. And this, by Allah, is truly a great and immense misguidance. We ask Allah, the All-Powerful, the All-Mighty, to take retribution for Islam and the Muslims against them, to suffice us against their evil, and to turn their plotting into destruction upon themselves and upon those who stand behind them among the Rafidah transgressors who harbor hatred against Ahlus Sunnah wal Jama'ah. O Allah, O Lord of Majesty and Honor, we seek Your protection against the throats of our enemies and the enemies of Your religion and the enemies of the Sunnah of Your Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم and the enemies of this blessed land, the Land of the Two Holy Mosques, the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. O Allah, protect it with Your protection. O Allah, protect its sky, its airspace, its land, and its sea, O Best of Protectors. O Allah, expose for us the evil schemes of the deceivers and the hypocrites, O All-Knowing, O All-Aware. O servants of Allah, send prayers and salutations upon the one whom we have been commanded to honor with prayers and salutations. O Allah, send Your blessings upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, as You sent Your blessings upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim. Indeed, You are the Most Praiseworthy, the Most Glorious. And bestow Your blessings upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, as You bestowed Your blessings upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim. Indeed, You are the Most Praiseworthy, the Most Glorious. O Allah, be pleased with the Rightly-Guided Caliphs, the rightly-guided Imams: Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman, and Ali, and with all of the companions of Your Prophet, his wives the mothers of the believers, and those who followed them in goodness. And include us among them through Your favor, generosity, and grace, O Lord of Majesty and Honor. O Allah, O Protector, O Guardian, protect Islam, the Muslims, and the lands of the Muslims. O Allah, protect Islam, the Muslims, and the lands of the Muslims from all harm and evil. O Allah, protect the Land of the Two Holy Mosques. O Allah, protect our brothers in Syria and in Palestine. O Allah, be with our brothers in Gaza, in Sudan, and in Yemen, O Best of Protectors. O Allah, Your enemies and the enemies of Your religion and the enemies of the Sunnah of Your Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم are lying in wait against us and against our Muslim brothers. So, O Allah, turn their plotting against their own throats and make it a destruction for them, O All-Powerful, O All-Wise, O All-Mighty. O Allah, protect our guardian, the Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques, King Salman ibn Abd al-Aziz, and his Crown Prince, Prince Muhammad ibn Salman. O Allah, extend their lives in Your obedience with health and well-being, support them and do not support anyone against them, grant them success, strengthen their resolve, and provide them with righteous and sincere advisors. O Allah, protect and strengthen the resolve of the men of our security forces, the men of defense, the men of the armed forces, and all security sectors. O Allah, grant them insight into our enemy and the enemy of our land, O Lord of Majesty and Honor. O Allah, forgive the Muslim men and women and the believing men and women, and especially our fathers and mothers. O Allah, reform the husbands and wives and the sons and daughters. O Allah, send down upon our homes and the homes of Muslims tranquility, happiness, harmony, love, and mercy, O Lord of Majesty and Honor. And keep away from us and from the homes of the Muslims discord, conflict, divorce, hatred, envy, the evil eye, sorcery, and illness, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy.
