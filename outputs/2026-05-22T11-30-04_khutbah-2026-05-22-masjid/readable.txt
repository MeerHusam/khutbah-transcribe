The Day of Arafah and Eid al-Adha are golden opportunities, fast on Arafah to erase two years of sins and offer Udhiyah to draw closer to Allah.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

DETAILED SUMMARY
================
This Jummah khutbah focuses on the immense spiritual significance of the Day of Arafah and Eid al-Adha. The khatib explains that the Day of Arafah is the greatest day of the year, during which Allah descends to the lowest heaven and takes pride in His servants, and that its blessings are not limited to Hajj pilgrims, since those at home can fast that day to have two years of sins expiated. He emphasises that Arafah is also the greatest day for supplication, urging the congregation to fill it with dhikr, du'a, charity, and prayer for the Ummah. In the second khutbah, the khatib turns to the Udhiyah (sacrificial offering), explaining that it is a confirmed Sunnah rooted in the tradition of Prophet Ibrahim, rewarding the offerer with a good deed for every hair of the animal, though it remains a Sunnah, not an obligation, for those who cannot afford it. The khutbah concludes with an extensive du'a for the acceptance of deeds, the wellbeing of pilgrims, the security and leadership of the country, the healing of the sick, and the unity and welfare of the Muslim Ummah worldwide.

FULL TRANSLATION
================
All praise is due to Allah, by Whose grace good deeds are completed, by Whose bounty and generosity ranks are elevated, good deeds are multiplied, and sins are forgiven. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner, Lord of all creation, the earths, and the heavens. And I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger, upon whom be the finest blessings and purest salutations, and upon his Companions, his wives, and those who follow them in goodness, and may Allah include us with them through His grace and generosity. Truly He is the Answerer of prayers. As for what follows: O servants of Allah, fear Allah the Almighty and seize the seasons of goodness so that you may be touched by the merciful blessings of the Most Mighty, Most Generous Lord. And know, may Allah have mercy on me and on you, that obedience to Allah is the believer's greatest capital. The truly fortunate one is he who invests it in earning his Lord's pleasure, and the loser is he who wastes it in amusement or heedlessness. O noble ones, in just a few days comes a day that is absolutely the best day in all of worldly life, it is the Day of Arafah. That is the day on which the Lord, Glorified and Exalted, descends to the lowest heaven in a manner befitting His majesty, and He boasts to His angels about the people standing at the gathering place, saying to them: 'Look at My servants, they have come to Me dishevelled and dusty.' This is truly the grace and favour of Allah, Glorified and Exalted. O noble ones, this day is not exclusive to the pilgrims standing on the plains of Arafah. Rather, Allah has blessings on this day that encompass those who are resident and not on pilgrimage. So whoever has not been destined to perform Hajj this year, a great door of drawing close to Allah has been opened for him, and that is the fast of Arafah. We praise Allah, Glorified and Exalted, for blessing us with the ten days of Dhul Hijjah, so that the believer may increase in nearness and elevation to his Lord, Glorified and Exalted. The Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم said regarding the virtue of the Day of Arafah: 'Fasting the Day of Arafah, I hope from Allah that it will expiate the sins of the year before it and the year after it.' Let us truly feel this immense favour from the Lord, Glorified and Exalted. A single day of fasting through which Allah wipes away the sins of two full years, what virtue could be greater, and what generosity could be wider than the generosity of the King, the All-Bestowing? O noble ones, the Day of Arafah is a day of supplication and a day when hopes are answered. The beloved Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم said: 'The best supplication is the supplication of the Day of Arafah, and the best thing I and the prophets before me have said: There is no god but Allah alone, with no partner, to Him belongs all dominion and all praise, and He is over all things capable.' So make this day a day devoted entirely to worship. Fill its hours with remembrance and declaration of Allah's oneness. Fill its hours with dhikr and tahleel. Beseech Allah persistently in supplication, remember the rights of your Muslim brothers and sisters, and raise your hands in humble prayer that Allah reform the affairs of the Ummah and unite its word upon truth and guidance. O servants of Allah, on the Day of Arafah, Shaytan despairs of things he does not despair of on other days, because of what he witnesses of the descent of mercy and the forgiveness of sins. So O you who hope for the forgiveness of your sins, and O you whose soul yearns for the elevation of your rank, fast this day, seek your reward with the Lord, and multiply your righteous deeds on it: prayer, charity, maintaining ties of kinship, supplication, and remembrance of Allah. For righteous deeds during these days carry immense weight in the sight of Allah. We ask Allah to grant us and you the arrival of these blessed seasons in this year and every year, with righteous deeds that He is pleased with, while we are in good health and well-being, and while Islam and the Muslims are in goodness and well-being. I have said what you have heard, and I seek forgiveness from Allah for myself and for you, for our parents, our brothers and sisters, our neighbours, and our relatives, so seek His forgiveness and turn to Him in repentance. For He is the Most Forgiving, the Most Merciful. All praise is due to Allah, Who has legislated for us rites through which we draw closer to Him, and has established for us acts of worship as servitude before Him, Glorified and Exalted. We praise Him, Glorified be He, for making Eid al-Adha a landmark of guidance and a symbol of Tawhid and taqwa, and I bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger, who sacrificed two white-and-black horned rams, and established this great rite for his Ummah. May peace and blessings of Allah be upon him in abundance. As for what follows: Fear Allah, O servants of Allah, and know, may Allah have mercy on me and on you, that the slaughtering of the Udhiyah on the Day of Sacrifice, which is the greatest day of the year, is among the most exalted acts of drawing near to Allah and the greatest acts of obedience by which the capable believer draws close to his Lord, Glorified and Exalted, on this blessed and magnificent day. It is the Sunnah of your father Ibrahim, peace be upon him. The beloved Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم was asked about it and said: 'It is the Sunnah of your father Ibrahim,' and they said: 'What do we gain from it, O Rasulullah?' He said: 'For every hair, a good deed.' And alongside this immense virtue, it is important to clarify that the Udhiyah is a confirmed Sunnah for those who are able, and it is not obligatory upon one who cannot afford it or is in financial hardship. For our religion is a religion of ease and mercy, 'Allah does not burden any soul beyond its capacity', so whoever is financially at ease, let him proceed with this act of worship seeking reward, and whoever is in difficulty, there is no blame upon him. For Allah accepts from His servant his sincere intention and his genuine turning to Him. 'Indeed, deeds are judged by intentions.' And our beloved Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم himself sacrificed on behalf of himself and his Ummah. O servants of Allah, the wise Shariah has set guidelines for this rite. Whoever intends to offer the Udhiyah, let it be from livestock animals, free from apparent defects, sound from anything that diminishes its value. Its time begins after the Eid prayer and extends to the end of the days of Tashriq. We ask Allah, Glorified and Exalted, to grant us and you the ability to perform the righteous deeds that please Him, and to make us and you among those whose lives are long and whose deeds are good. O Allah, accept from us and from all Muslims their deeds and their acts of worship, and make them purely for Your noble countenance, bringing us closer to You in the gardens of bliss. O Allah, help us to remember You, to be grateful to You, and to worship You well. O Allah, accept the Hajj of the pilgrims. O Allah, bring them safely to their destination and return them safely to their families, enriched and with their efforts accepted, O Lord of Majesty and Honour. O Allah, grant us and them success in what You love and are pleased with. O Allah, grant us success and accept from us and from them, O Lord of Majesty and Honour, O Lord of immense bounty. O Allah, O Most Mighty, strengthen Islam and the Muslims, and humiliate shirk and the polytheists. Make this land safe, tranquil, prosperous, and generous, as well as all the lands of the Muslims. O Allah, complete and perpetuate security and safety upon us and upon all the lands of the Muslims, O Lord of Majesty and Honour. O Allah, grant success to and preserve our guardian, the Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques, and his Crown Prince. O Allah, grant them success in what You love and are pleased with. O Allah, aid them and guide them in their actions and their words. O Allah, grant them success and guide them in their words and their actions, O Lord of Majesty and Honour. O Allah, grant success to and preserve our security forces. O Allah, grant success to and preserve our security forces. O Allah, grant success to those who serve Your guests, O Lord of Majesty and Honour. O Allah, aid them, aid them, guide them, and make them aware of Your enemy and the enemy of our land, O Lord of Majesty and Honour. O Allah, relieve the debt of the indebted Muslims, and heal and cure our sick and the sick among the Muslims. O Allah, heal and cure our sick and the sick among the Muslims. O Allah, spread health and well-being throughout their bodies, O Most Merciful, O Most Compassionate. O Allah, O Lord of Majesty and Honour, forgive the Muslim men and Muslim women, the believing men and believing women, and grant special forgiveness to the fathers and mothers among them. O Allah, reform the husbands and wives, the sons and daughters. O Allah, O Lord of Majesty and Honour, reform the conditions of Islam and the Muslims everywhere, O Lord of the Worlds. O Allah, send down upon us, our homes, and the homes of the Muslims tranquility, peace of mind, love, harmony, health, well-being, and happiness. And keep away from us and from the homes of the Muslims discord, conflict, divorce, hatred, enmity, black magic, the evil eye, and illness, O Most Merciful of all who show mercy. O Allah, send Your blessings and peace upon Your servant and Messenger Muhammad, and upon the family of Muhammad, as You sent Your blessings upon Ibrahim and the family of Ibrahim. Indeed You are and peace be upon all the Messengers. And all praise is due to Allah, Lord of all the Worlds.
