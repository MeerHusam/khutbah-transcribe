This Friday's Khutbah focused on the blessed Day of Arafah and the rite of Udhiyah (sacrificial offering). Key takeaways: fasting on the Day of Arafah expiates the sins of two years, and it's a prime time for du'a and worship. The second part reminded us that offering the Udhiyah is a confirmed Sunnah with great reward — for every hair of the animal, a good deed is recorded!

DETAILED SUMMARY
================
The Khatib opened by urging the congregation to seize the blessed seasons of worship, describing the Day of Arafah as the best day of the worldly year, on which Allah descends to the lowest heaven and boasts to His angels about His servants. He emphasised that the blessings of this day are not limited to the pilgrims at Arafah, but extend to all Muslims through fasting, remembrance, supplication, and good deeds, citing the Hadith that fasting on Arafah expiates two years of sins. The second part of the Khutbah addressed the rite of Udhiyah, explaining that it is a confirmed Sunnah of the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم following the tradition of Ibrahim عليه السلام, carrying immense reward — a good deed for every hair — while clarifying it is not obligatory on those without means. The Khatib outlined the Islamic conditions for the sacrifice and concluded with an extensive du'a covering acceptance of good deeds, the pilgrims, the safety and prosperity of Muslim lands, the health of the sick, the guidance of Muslim leaders, and the unity and well-being of the Ummah worldwide.

FULL TRANSLATION
================
All praise is due to Allah, by Whose grace good deeds are completed, by Whose bounty and generosity ranks are elevated, good deeds are multiplied, and sins are forgiven. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner, the Lord of all creation, the earths, and the heavens. And I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger, upon whom be the finest of prayers and the purest of salutations, and upon his companions, his wives, and those who follow them in righteousness. May Allah include us with them through His grace and generosity — He is indeed the Answerer of prayers. As for what follows: O servants of Allah, fear Allah, the Mighty and Exalted, and seize the blessed seasons so that you may receive the divine outpourings from the Most Mighty, Most Generous Lord. Know — may Allah have mercy on me and on you — that obedience is the believer's true capital. The truly blessed person is the one who invests it in seeking the pleasure of his Lord, while the loser is the one who squanders it in amusement or heedlessness. O noble ones, in just a few days comes a day that is, without any doubt, the best day in this worldly life — it is the Day of Arafah. That is the day on which the Lord, Mighty and Exalted, descends to the lowest heaven in a manner befitting His majesty, and He boasts to His angels about the pilgrims standing at Arafah, saying to them: 'Look at My servants — they have come to Me dishevelled and dusty.' Such is the bounty of Allah, Mighty and Exalted. O noble ones, this day is not exclusive to the pilgrims standing on the plain of Arafah; rather, Allah's divine outpourings on this day encompass all who are travelling and all who are staying at home. Whoever was not destined to perform Hajj this year has had a great door of drawing near to Allah opened for him — and that is the fast of the Day of Arafah. We praise Allah, Mighty and Exalted, for blessing us with the ten days of Dhul-Hijjah, so that the believer may increase in closeness and elevation to his Lord, Mighty and Exalted. The Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم said regarding the virtue of the Day of Arafah: 'Fasting the Day of Arafah — I hope from Allah that it expiates the sins of the year before it and the year after it.' Let us truly feel the weight of this favour from the Lord, Mighty and Exalted — a single day of fasting through which Allah wipes away the sins of two full years. What greater grace is there, and what generosity is wider than the generosity of the King, the Bestower? O noble ones, the Day of Arafah is a day of supplication and a day when hopes are answered. The beloved Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم said: 'The best supplication is the supplication of the Day of Arafah, and the best of what I and the Prophets before me have said is: There is no god but Allah alone, with no partner — to Him belongs all dominion and all praise, and He is over all things Omnipotent.' So make this day a day of devoted worship. Fill its hours with remembrance and with the proclamation of tahleel. Fill its hours with remembrance and tahleel. Beseech Allah earnestly in supplication, remember the rights of your Muslim brothers and sisters, and raise your hands in humble prayer that Allah rectify the affairs of the Ummah and unite it upon truth and guidance. O servants of Allah, the devil despairs on the Day of Arafah in a way he does not despair on any other day, because of what he witnesses of the descending of mercies and the forgiveness of sins. So O you who hope for the forgiveness of your sins, and O you whose soul yearns for the elevation of your rank — fast this day, seek your reward with the Lord, and increase in righteous deeds: prayer, charity, maintaining ties of kinship, supplication, and remembrance of Allah. For righteous deeds in these days carry immense weight in the sight of Allah. We ask Allah to allow us and you to reach these blessed seasons every year upon righteous deeds that please Him, while we are in good health and well-being, and while Islam and the Muslims are in a state of goodness and well-being. I have said what you have heard, and I seek forgiveness from Allah for myself and for you, for our parents, our brothers and sisters, our neighbours and our relatives — so seek His forgiveness and turn to Him in repentance, for He is the Most Forgiving, the Most Merciful. All praise is due to Allah, Who has prescribed for us acts of worship through which we draw near to Him, and established for us rites of devotion before His Majesty. We praise Him, Glorified be He, for making Eid al-Adha a landmark of guidance and a symbol of tawheed and taqwa. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner. And I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger, who sacrificed two white-and-black horned rams, and established this great rite for his Ummah. May the finest prayers and most complete peace and blessings be upon him. As for what follows: Fear Allah, O servants of Allah, and know — may Allah have mercy on me and on you — that the slaughter of the sacrifice on the Day of Nahr, which is the greatest day of the year, is one of the most honoured acts of drawing near to Allah and among the greatest acts of obedience that the capable believer performs to come close to his Lord, Mighty and Exalted, on this noble day. And it is the Sunnah of your father Ibrahim, peace be upon him. The beloved Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم was asked about it and said: 'The Sunnah of your father Ibrahim.' They said: 'What do we receive from it, O Rasulullah?' He said: 'For every hair, a good deed.' Despite this immense virtue, it must be clarified that the Udhiyah is a confirmed Sunnah upon those who are able, and it is not obligatory upon one who lacks the means or is in financial hardship. For our religion is one of ease and mercy — 'Allah does not burden a soul beyond its capacity' — so whoever is financially comfortable should come forward and offer this act of devotion seeking Allah's reward, and whoever is in hardship, there is no blame upon them. For Allah accepts from His servant his sincere intention and his genuine turning to Him. Indeed, deeds are judged by intentions. Our beloved Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم sacrificed on behalf of himself and his Ummah. O servants of Allah, Islamic law has laid down clear guidelines for this rite. Whoever wishes to offer the Udhiyah, it must be from among the an'aam livestock, free from apparent defects and sound of condition, not suffering from anything that diminishes its value. Its time begins after the Eid prayer and extends until the end of the days of Tashreeq. We ask Allah, Mighty and Exalted, to grant us and you success in performing the righteous deeds that please Him, and to make us among those whose lives are long and whose deeds are good. O Allah, accept from us and from all Muslims their deeds and their acts of worship, and make them purely for Your noble sake, drawing us closer to You in the gardens of bliss. O Allah, help us to remember You, to thank You, and to worship You well. O Allah, accept the Hajj of the pilgrims. O Allah, bring them safely to their destination and return them safely to their families, having gained much and with their efforts accepted, O Lord of Majesty and Honour. O Allah, grant us and them success in what You love and what pleases You. O Allah, grant us success and accept from us and from them, O Lord of Majesty and Honour, O Lord of immense grace. O Allah, O Most Mighty, honour Islam and the Muslims, and humble shirk and the polytheists. Make this country safe, secure, prosperous, and generous, and likewise all the lands of the Muslims. O Allah, complete and perpetuate upon us and upon all Muslim lands security and safety, O Lord of Majesty and Honour. O Allah, grant success to and protect our guardian, the Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques, and his Crown Prince. O Allah, guide them to what You love and what pleases You. O Allah, aid them and direct them rightly in their actions and their words. O Lord of Majesty and Honour. O Allah, grant success to and protect the men of our security forces. O Allah, grant success to and protect the men of our security forces. O Allah, grant success to those serving Your guests, O Lord of Majesty and Honour. O Allah, aid them. O Allah, aid them, guide them aright, and grant them insight into Your enemy and the enemy of our land, O Lord of Majesty and Honour. O Allah, relieve the debts of the indebted among the Muslims. Heal and cure our sick and the sick among the Muslims. O Allah, heal and cure our sick and the sick among the Muslims. O Allah, spread health and well-being throughout their bodies, O Most Merciful, O Most Compassionate. O Allah, O Lord of Majesty and Honour, forgive the Muslim men and Muslim women, the believing men and believing women, and especially among them the fathers and the mothers. O Allah, rectify the spouses — husbands and wives — and the sons and daughters. O Allah, O Lord of Majesty and Honour, set aright the affairs of Islam and the Muslims in every place, O Lord of all the worlds. O Allah, send down upon us, our homes, and the homes of the Muslims tranquillity, peace of heart, love, harmony, health, well-being, and happiness. And keep far from us and from the homes of the Muslims discord, conflict, divorce, hatred, malice, sorcery, the evil eye, and illness, O Most Merciful of the merciful. O Allah, send Your prayers and peace upon Your servant and Messenger Muhammad, and upon the family of Muhammad, just as You sent Your prayers upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim. Indeed You are And peace be upon the Messengers. And all praise is due to Allah, Lord of all the worlds.
