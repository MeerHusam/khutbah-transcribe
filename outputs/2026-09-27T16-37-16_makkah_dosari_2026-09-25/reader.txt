By this it is understood, O servants of Allah, by this it is understood, O servants of Allah, that loftiness of aspiration is not an intellectual luxury nor a philosophical abstraction, but rather it is a path to achieving great goals and a means to attaining the pleasure of the All-Knowing King.

This increases our certainty of the enormity of the trust we carry. Preserving the homeland is an act of devotion, defending it and its sanctities is an act of worship, unity of ranks is a protection, and unity of word for the sake of the land is a safeguarding.

فحافظوا على أمن وطنكم واستقراره وازدهاره وكونوا على قدر رؤيته وتطلعاته واجتهدوا في خدمته كل بحسب موقعه وقدرته.

So preserve the security, stability, and prosperity of your homeland, be worthy of its vision and aspirations, and strive in serving it, each according to his position and capability.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

حتى يكون كل واحد منا لبنة بناء ومصدر خير ونماء مسهما في مسيرة وطن طموح تتعاقب أجياله على صيانة مكتسباته ومواصلة منجزاته وصناعة مستقبله.

So that every one of us becomes a building block and a source of goodness and growth, contributing to the journey of an ambitious homeland whose successive generations preserve its gains, continue its achievements, and shape its future.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

عباد الله، ألا وصلوا على صاحب الخلق العظيم نبيكم محمد الكريم فقد أمركم الله بذلك في كتابه المبين فقال:

O servants of Allah, send salutations upon the one of great character, your Prophet Muhammad the Noble, for Allah has commanded you to do so in His clear Book, and He said:

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

إن الله وملائكته يصلون على النبي يا أيها الذين آمنوا صلوا عليه وسلموا تسليما.

And bless Muhammad and the family of Muhammad as You blessed Ibrahim and the family of Ibrahim in all the worlds. Verily You are the Most Praiseworthy, the Most Glorious.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

اللهم أعز الإسلام والمسلمين واحم حوزة الدين وانصر دينك وكتابك وسنة نبيك صلى الله عليه وسلم وعبادك المؤمنين.

Protect this land in its creed and its faith. Protect this land in its creed and its faith, its territory and its sanctities, its security and its stability, its prosperity and its flourishing.

Protect it and protect the lands of the Muslims from the plotting of plotters and the aggression of aggressors.
