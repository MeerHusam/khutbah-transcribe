DETAILED SUMMARY
================
This Friday khutbah centers on the immense blessing of security, describing how it enables worship, learning, work, and family life to flourish, and drawing on Quranic verses and hadiths to illustrate its profound value. The khatib honors the armed forces and security personnel who sacrifice their rest and comfort to protect the nation, citing prophetic traditions that exalt the merit of those who stand guard in the path of Allah. A major portion of the khutbah addresses the sanctity of the Two Holy Cities, Makkah and al-Madinah, and strongly condemns the Houthi missile and drone attacks on Makkah as a monstrous crime comparable to the campaign of Abrahah the Abyssinian. The second part calls on the congregation to express gratitude for security through responsible conduct, including not filming or sharing news of security incidents before official confirmation, and praises the Kingdom's long history of supporting Muslim causes worldwide. The khutbah closes with an extended supplication asking Allah to preserve security, protect the holy sites, grant victory to the oppressed in Palestine, al-Sham, Yemen, and Sudan, and bring ruin upon all who threaten the Muslims.

FULL TRANSLATION
================
All praise is due to Allah, the Sovereign, the Bestower, vast in His grace and benevolence. He made security a tranquility for hearts and a stability for the homeland, and He commanded the preservation of souls and the protection of sanctities and rights. We praise Him, Glorified is He, and thank Him for His countless blessings and His favors that cannot be fully enumerated. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, alone with no partner. And I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger. May Allah's peace, blessings, and salutations be upon him, upon his family, his companions, and those who follow them in righteousness. May Allah be pleased with them all through His grace and generosity. He is the Most Generous of the generous. As for what follows: I counsel myself and you to have taqwa of Allah, Mighty and Majestic, for it is the counsel of Allah to those who came before and those who come after. Brothers in faith, among the greatest blessings of Allah upon His servants is the blessing of security. Through it, souls find rest, hearts find tranquility, blood and rights are protected, and the interests of both religion and worldly life are set aright. Under its shade, the one who prays worships his Lord, Mighty and Majestic. Under its shade, the student seeks his knowledge, the worker strives for his provision, and families rest peacefully, assured in the safety of their lives, their children, their belongings, and their honor. And because of the greatness of this immense blessing, Allah, Mighty and Majestic, reminded Quraysh of it as a favor upon them, saying, Mighty and Majestic, He thus paired the blessing of security with the blessing of food, because people need sustenance by which they live and security in which they find peace. Our beloved Messenger, salla Allahu alayhi wa sallam, said: Whoever among you wakes up in the morning safe in his home, healthy in his body, and having food for his day, it is as though the whole world has been gathered for him. Reported by al-Tirmidhi. So reflect, O servants of Allah, may Allah have mercy on me and on you, how many blessings we grow accustomed to and therefore fail to appreciate their true worth. We go out to our work and return to our homes and gather with our families and relatives, yet we do not truly know the value of security until we remember that its loss disrupts livelihoods, tears families apart, and burdens hearts with fear and pain. O Muslims, among the means by which security is preserved, by the grace of Allah, Mighty and Majestic, are the men of our armed forces and the defenders stationed at the frontiers. They guard the borders, defend the land and its people, stay awake while others sleep, and endure hardship so that people may feel safe in their homes. The Prophet, salla Allahu alayhi wa sallam, said: One day of ribat in the path of Allah is better than the world and everything in it. Reported by al-Bukhari. And he, salla Allahu alayhi wa sallam, said: One day and one night of ribat is better than fasting a month and standing in prayer throughout it. Reported by Muslim. Alongside them, the men of our security forces maintain security within the country, protect lives and property, combat crime, safeguard rights, and restore what has been wrongfully taken to those who deserve it. The beloved Messenger, salla Allahu alayhi wa sallam, said: Two eyes will never be touched by the Fire: an eye that wept out of fear of Allah, and an eye that spent the night standing guard in the path of Allah. Reported by al-Tirmidhi. May Allah reward them with the best of rewards for what they give and sacrifice. O servants of Allah, among the greatest sanctities that must be honored and revered are the sanctities of the Two Holy Mosques, Makkah al-Mukarramah and al-Madinah al-Munawwarah. In Makkah stands the Sacred House, toward which the faces of Muslims turn in their prayers, and to which pilgrims and those performing Umrah come from all over. And al-Madinah is the city to which the Prophet, salla Allahu alayhi wa sallam, made his Hijra, and in it stands his noble mosque, salla Allahu alayhi wa sallam. Allah, Mighty and Majestic, said concerning His Sacred House, And the Prophet, salla Allahu alayhi wa sallam, said concerning Makkah al-Mukarramah: This land is one that Allah has made sacred. Reported by al-Bukhari. And the beloved Messenger, salla Allahu alayhi wa sallam, said concerning al-Madinah: Whoever intends harm against the people of al-Madinah, Allah will dissolve him as salt dissolves in water. Reported by Muslim. Reverence for the sanctity of the Two Holy Cities does not stop at words alone, but is expressed in protecting those who travel to them and safeguarding the security of the pilgrims, those performing Umrah, and all visitors. Every aggression against the innocent, or every threat to the security of the holy sites and those who make their way to them, is a violation of this sanctity and an assault on what Allah has commanded to be protected and honored. O Muslims, among the most heinous and vile of crimes is what the enemies of Allah and of this religion, the Houthis, have dared to do: violating the sanctity of Makkah al-Mukarramah and threatening the security of Allah's Sacred Land, launching their missiles and drones toward it. The Grand Mufti of the Kingdom, His Eminence Sheikh Salih al-Fawzan, may Allah preserve and protect him, likened this despicable act to the act of Abrahah the Abyssinian, and noted that they have repeated the history of Abrahah in targeting Makkah al-Mukarramah with evil. How immense is their audacity against the sanctities of Allah. They raise the slogans of Islam, then direct the weapons of their war toward the Qiblah of the Muslims, the Sacred Land of Allah, and the place to which the hearts of the believers are drawn. What contradiction between claim and action could be more glaring than this? And what crime is more vile than terrorizing the innocent and violating the sanctity of the sacred and inviolable House that Allah has exalted? Allah has immortalized the account of a people who targeted His House with evil and made clear their fate, saying, Mighty and Majestic, And He, Glorified and Exalted, said, Makkah al-Mukarramah is the sanctuary of Allah, and the Kaaba is the Qiblah of the Muslims. Whoever targets it with evil has dared to transgress against a sanctity that Allah, Mighty and Majestic, has made great. The crime of these Houthis in threatening its security is not merely an assault on a country. It is a transgression against a sacred site to which the hearts of over a billion Muslims, in every corner of the east and west, are attached. O servants of Allah, let us thank Allah, Mighty and Majestic, for the blessing of security and for what this blessed state provides, through its rulers, princes, security forces, and defenders, for all the effort and dedication they pour into preserving the security of these two holy cities, may Allah protect and watch over them and their people and all those residing in them, and for everything they give and sacrifice with every means at their disposal in defending this land. Let us thank Allah, Mighty and Majestic, for the blessing of security, and let us fulfill our responsibility in preserving it. Let none of us be a cause of harm, a helper in corruption, or a channel for a rumor that unsettles the people. O Allah, perpetuate for us security and faith, and protect us from every evil and harm. I say what you hear and I seek forgiveness from Allah for myself and for you, for our parents, for our spouses, for our children, for our brothers and sisters, for our relatives, for our neighbors, and for all Muslims. Seek His forgiveness, for He is the All-Forgiving, the Most Merciful. All praise is due to Allah for His benevolence. Thanks and gratitude are His for His guidance and His favor. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, alone with no partner, in exaltation and reverence of His Majesty. And I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger. May Allah's blessings be upon him, upon his family, his companions, his brethren, and those who follow them in righteousness. May Allah be pleased with them all through His grace and generosity. He is the Most Generous of the generous. As for what follows: My counsel to myself and to you is to have taqwa of Allah, Mighty and Majestic. So fear Allah, that you may receive mercy. O servants of Allah, gratitude for the blessing of security does not rest in words alone. It shows in deeds and conduct: in respecting people's rights, preserving property, cooperating with the security forces in repelling danger, and reporting to the relevant authorities whatever calls for their intervention through their official channels. It is not a responsible act, O servants of Allah, when a security incident occurs, to publish it, photograph it, or circulate news of it before it has been verified and before the official authorized body has made an announcement. The safety of lives and the protection of security take priority over rushing to photograph, publish, and broadcast news. Let each one of us be a support for good, a source of reassurance, and a guardian of trust in the station where Allah, Mighty and Majestic, has placed him. Among what should be recalled, O servants of Allah, is that this country, and all praise and gratitude belong to Allah, is a generous and giving land. Its rulers have stood in support of the causes of Islam and Muslims throughout history, from its founding under its Imam, King Abdulaziz, and his forebears before him. Let us praise Allah, Mighty and Majestic, that its constitution is the Quran and the Sunnah, and that supporting the causes of Muslims in every corner of the east and west, and their importance, as you know, the cause of Palestine and the support of their brothers in Sudan, in Yemen, in every place, and in al-Sham, is a weighty responsibility they carry. They spare no effort or sacrifice for that purpose. And likewise, as you know, this land, and all praise and gratitude belong to Allah, has opened its doors to many from the east and the west. You will not find a land like it in this inhabited world that has given so generously and bestowed so much upon those residing in it, treating them indeed as its guests. So let us praise Allah, Mighty and Majestic, for this blessing. Let us support one another in preserving its security and stability, and let us thank Allah, Mighty and Majestic, and supplicate for our rulers and our security forces, asking for their success, steadfastness, protection, and well-being. With this, send your prayers and salutations, may Allah have mercy on me and on you, upon the one whom Allah has commanded us to honor with prayers and salutations, for Allah, Mighty and Majestic, says, And he, salla Allahu alayhi wa sallam, said: Whoever sends one salutation upon me, Allah will send ten blessings upon him in return. O Allah, send Your blessings upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, as You sent Your blessings upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim. You are indeed Praiseworthy, Most Glorious. And bestow Your blessings, O Allah, upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, as You bestowed Your blessings upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim. You are indeed Praiseworthy, Most Glorious. And be pleased, O Allah, with the Rightly Guided Caliphs, the rightly directed Imams: Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman, and Ali, and with all the companions of Your Prophet, his wives the Mothers of the Believers, and those who follow them in righteousness. And grant us Your pleasure alongside them, through Your grace, generosity, and benevolence, O Most Generous of the generous. O Allah, O Almighty, grant strength and honor to Islam and the Muslims. O Allah, O Guardian, protect Islam, the Muslims, and the lands of the Muslims from every evil and harm, O Lord of all the worlds. O Allah, perpetuate for us security and safety, O Lord of all the worlds, and for all the lands of the Muslims. O Allah, perpetuate security and safety upon our land and upon all the lands of the Muslims. O Allah, be with our vulnerable brothers and sisters in Palestine. O Allah, grant peace and safety to the people of al-Sham, the people of Yemen, the people of Sudan, and every place, O Lord of all the worlds. O Allah, deal with those who harbor ill intentions toward Islam and the Muslims. O Allah, O Powerful, O Almighty, O All-Knowing, O All-Wise, we ask You by this great and blessed day, for You are fully aware of all things. O Allah, whoever intends harm against our land, our sacred sites, our rulers, our security forces, and the unity of our ranks, or seeks to terrorize the innocent, O Allah, turn their scheming into their own destruction and ruin. O Powerful, O Almighty. O Allah, we seek Your protection against the threats of our enemies, the enemies of Your religion, and the enemies of our land. O All-Knowing, O Almighty. O Allah, deal with the aggressing Houthis, and with the Persians, and with the aggressing and spiteful Zionists. O Powerful, O Almighty. O Allah, O Possessor of Majesty and Honor, forgive the Muslim men and Muslim women, the believing men and believing women. Single out among them especially the fathers and mothers. And rectify, O Allah, the husbands and wives, the sons and daughters. O Most Merciful of the merciful. O Allah, relieve the distress of those among the Muslims who are burdened with grief. Settle the debts of us and our Muslim brothers and sisters. Heal our sick and the sick among all Muslims.
