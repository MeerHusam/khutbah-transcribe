This Friday's Khutbah was about making the most of Ramadan as a season of spiritual renewal, forgiveness, and action. The Khatib also spoke passionately about the suffering of Muslims in Palestine, Kashmir, and Chechnya, urging us to pray, give, and stand together as an Ummah. He also reminded parents to look after their children and warned against the harmful content flooding our screens this Ramadan — let's guard our families! 🤲

DETAILED SUMMARY
================
The Khatib opened by praising the blessings of Ramadan as the greatest of spiritual seasons, citing authentic Hadiths on the gates of Paradise being opened, devils chained, and sins forgiven for those who fast and pray with sincere faith. He called on the Ummah to transform this month into a turning point — moving from division toward unity upon the Quran and Sunnah — and to reclaim the civilizational glory Islam achieved in Ramadan throughout history. A significant portion of the sermon was devoted to the crises facing Muslims in Palestine, Kashmir, and especially Chechnya, condemning the silence of the world in the face of massacres and calling on Islamic governments and international bodies to take decisive action. The Khatib also addressed internal challenges: the proliferation of immoral satellite and media content during Ramadan that trivializes the month, calling on leaders and communities to resist it; the obligation of Zakat and charity; and the responsibility of parents to supervise their children. He concluded by urging all Muslims to intensify Quran recitation, repentance, and supplication, particularly as the middle ten days — the days of forgiveness — begin, and reminded the congregation to increase their prayers upon the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم.

FULL TRANSLATION
================
All praise is due to Allah, who blessed us with seasons of goodness, and distinguished the month of Ramadan with virtue, honor, blessings, and spiritual gifts. He encouraged in it the performance of acts of obedience and the increase of deeds that draw one closer to Him. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner — a testimony that benefits its speaker in life and after death. And I bear witness that our Prophet Muhammad is the servant of Allah and His Messenger, the best of all creation. May Allah send His peace and blessings upon him, his family, and his companions — those of virtue and nobility — and upon those who followed them in excellence, for as long as the heavens and earth endure. And may He send abundant peace.

To proceed: O Muslim men and women, O fasting brothers and sisters — fear Allah, the Blessed and Exalted, and be grateful to Him for the great occasions He has prepared for you. These are occasions that anchor faith in the hearts, stir deep and overflowing emotions in the soul, increasing acts of obedience and narrowing the avenues of evil and transgression for individuals and communities alike. They impart Islamic lessons and faith-strengthening impressions of unity, solidarity, purity, righteousness, connection, happiness, goodness, patience, sacrifice, and dignity. They are generous shades, sweet springs, and mighty fortresses for the obedient — and irreplaceable opportunities for sinners and the negligent to begin a new page in the record of their lives.

O Muslim community: Among the greatest of these occasions in terms of time, the most significant in terms of value, and the most profound in terms of impact, is the fragrance of these bright and blessed days and these luminous, distinguished nights in this noble month. We draw from its pure spring, drink deeply from its waters, taste its sweet flowing streams, and breathe the fragrance of its scent. It is the month of multiplied good deeds, forgiveness of sins and misdeeds, elevation of ranks and removal of stumbles. It is the season of trade and profit, and in it souls race toward the lands of joy.

'O you who believe, fasting has been prescribed for you as it was prescribed for those before you, so that you may become righteous.'

In the authentic report, Rasulullah صلى الله عليه وسلم said: 'When Ramadan enters, the gates of Paradise are opened, the gates of Hell are closed, and the devils are chained. A caller calls out: O seeker of good, come forward! O seeker of evil, hold back! And Allah has those He frees from the Fire — and that is every night.' 'Whoever fasts it and stands in prayer during it with faith and seeking reward, his past sins will be forgiven.' This was reported by both al-Bukhari and Muslim from the Hadith of Abu Hurayrah رضي الله عنه.

O brothers in Islam, O nation of fasting and night prayer: It is truly fitting for the Islamic Ummah — which lives today in one of the darkest of its times and one of the most dangerous phases of its life — to make this month a turning point from a life of division and disagreement to holding fast to the Book of Allah and the Sunnah of His Messenger صلى الله عليه وسلم, and the methodology of the righteous predecessors, may Allah have mercy on them. This month should be a phase of change for the better in every domain of the lives of individuals, nations, and communities. Through that, the Ummah can restore its glorious history and its radiant, magnificent civilization — a history rich with glories and victories achieved in this noble month.

O nation of Islam: How much those who are protective of the affairs of their Ummah, who follow its pains closely, wished that the blessed month of Ramadan would arrive and find the wounds of the Ummah healed, its affairs corrected, its tragedies ended, and the tears dried from its eyes. But with deep and painful sorrow, the tragedies of our Ummah continue, its conditions remain inflamed, its wounds still bleed, and the blood of its steadfast sons flows amidst arrogance and recklessness, in the midst of a world's silence, an international hush, and a media blackout.

What is the news of those standing firm on the soil of mujahid Palestine? How do the sons of the blessed land receive this noble month? What is the news of the first of the two Qiblahs and the night journey destination of the master of all creation? May Allah bring comfort to eyes and ease to hearts through the breaking of its captivity and the nearness of its liberation — after the brothers of apes and pigs have done to it what causes foreheads to sweat with shame. 'And they strive to spread corruption in the earth, and Allah does not love the corruptors.'

What is the news on your soil, O wounded Muslim Kashmir? Where the hateful pagans continue to kill and displace your Muslim brothers.

O nation of Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم: The burning page and the inflamed issue of these days is the cause and tragedy of our brothers in faith in the land of Chechnya. It is the deepest wound and the most dangerous situation. The atheists have acted against them with defiance, arrogance, transgression, and insistence — plotting a great plot against their creed, their resources, and their land. Our brothers are there with homes destroyed, buildings burned, and the people left without shelter, food, water, or medicine. The city of Grozny, which was once considered the bride of the Caucasus, has today become a city of ghosts. Peace of Allah be upon those who stand firm in jihad.

The world has deafened its ears to the whiz of shells and the heat of bombs, and the spilling of Muslim blood in one of the most horrific scenes in contemporary history. Which eye can afford to withhold floods of tears while seeing the condition of Muslims in this tragic situation? No one asks about the dire conditions of thousands of the displaced, the uprooted, and the refugees. Should Muslims stand with their arms crossed, in positions of weakness, as if the matter does not concern them?

What I fear most is that the curtain will be drawn over this issue, as happened to similar ones before — leaving our Islamic lands to fragment and their pieces scattered in the hands of enemies, with parts of their body severed. So sufficient for us is Allah, and what an excellent Trustee He is. To Allah we complain, and there is no power or might except with Allah the Most High, the Most Great.

In the name of the fasting and praying Muslims, we call upon all those concerned — Islamic governments, international bodies, and global organizations — to work diligently to stop the hideous massacres and vicious attacks against our Chechen brothers, which represent the most egregious violation of human rights and international norms — especially as these powers prepare to enter the third millennium while uttering hollow calls for human rights. Condemnation and denunciation are not enough; urgent and decisive action and firm measures are required to preserve human rights and to protect what remains of our brothers' existence and identity.

Blessed are the praiseworthy efforts of the Land of the Two Holy Mosques — may Allah protect it — in supporting the causes of Muslims, alleviating their suffering, and attending to their needs. May Allah place that in their scales of good deeds.

So be patient, O sons of Chechnya — your steadfastness has shaken the red tyrants even in their own days. And similar grief can be said about the many regions of the world where oppressed peoples and Muslim minorities live. But victory belongs to Islam and its people, and Ramadan is the season of victories. It is the duty of the Ummah to seize opportunities before they pass.

O brothers in creed and faith: In contrast to this dark and sorrowful picture, there is another painful and disturbing image — one that reflects the state of some who attribute themselves to Islam. It represents a strange distortion of identity and a loss of purpose and means, embodied by media channels, satellite networks, and information networks filled with content that blemishes the spiritual majesty and beauty of this month of faith. It is a display of a warped 'Ramadan menu' broadcast by foolish and frivolous people — in vulgar scenes and reprehensible spectacles — transforming the spiritual sanctity of the noble month into a season of triviality and shamelessness, enticing those seeking forbidden pleasures under the claim that this lightens the burden of fasting. And how wretched is what they claim.

This disgraceful excess in displaying cheap entertainment to the Ummah requires firm stances that rein it in and curtail its harm, so that this moral rot does not eat into the souls of our Muslim generations, exposing the entire Ummah to civilizational collapse — at a time when it is most in need of holding firmly to its constants and principles in a world turbulent with challenges and changes, and in an era dominated by materialism and alluring temptations.

The concerned members of the Ummah have called for the necessity of resisting this satellite excess and this media and information challenge — lest it become an entry point through which destructive negative influences seep into the values and morals of the Ummah. It is not enough for throats to be hoarse with appeals; this must be reinforced with measures and tools that restrain those reckless individuals, warn the Ummah against them, and establish suitable Islamic alternatives to preserve the moral and ethical fabric of the Ummah.

So O leaders of the Muslims: The opportunity is yours in this noble month to return to Allah and apply the Sharia of Allah on Allah's earth among Allah's servants, and to throw your full weight in international forums and global conferences to guarantee the rights of Muslims and Islamic peoples.

O brothers in Allah: Remember, while you enjoy the blessings of fasting and night prayer in a uniquely spiritual atmosphere — soaring through rare horizons of faith — that you have brothers who are oppressed in their religion. Share in their hopes and pains, share in their joys and sorrows, extend a helping hand to them, and do not forget them in your charity and your supplications.

Here is a reminder for the wealthy Muslims and those of means and capacity: Fear Allah regarding the wealth of Allah which He has given you and made you stewards of — so give generously and do not be miserly, give freely and do not withhold. For Ramadan is the opportunity for return, generosity, giving, and charity.

Nor should one fail to mention in this context the obligation of Zakat, which some people — may Allah guide them — have been negligent about. It is paired with Salah in the Book of Allah عز وجل and is the third pillar of Islam.

O Muslim sisters: Fasting trains one toward goodness, virtue, and a righteous life, and leads women along the path of modesty and chastity. Whatever is seen of manifestations of immodest display, unveiling, and mixing — this is evidence of little understanding of this great act of worship. So fear Allah, O Muslim women. Beware of the thieves of honor and the pirates of virtue and the callers to vice. Adorn yourselves with the hijab, modesty, and chastity, and beware of immodest display, unveiling, and free mixing.

And indeed among the phenomena worthy of remedy, O servants of Allah, is the phenomenon of some fathers and mothers falling short in following up on their sons and daughters — may Allah guide them — releasing them without supervision or accountability in this noble month. So fear Allah, O fathers — follow up on your sons and daughters and fulfill what Allah has made obligatory upon you. For He, the Exalted, has said: 'O you who believe, protect yourselves and your families from a fire.' And the Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم said: 'Every one of you is a shepherd and every one of you is responsible for his flock.' This was reported by both al-Bukhari and Muslim from the Hadith of Ibn 'Umar رضي الله عنهما.

We address in you your protective zeal, your manhood, your concern for your honor and those under your protection, and your duty on the Day of Resurrection.

Busy yourselves with remembrance of Allah, glorification, seeking forgiveness, supplication, giving charity and doing good. Make this month an opportunity for affection, love, righteousness, family ties, and forgiveness. Beware of severing ties, animosity, hatred, envy, and malice. Show Allah the good in yourselves. Renew your repentance from your sins and fulfill its conditions — so that perhaps Allah may pardon your sins and make you among those shown mercy and freed from the Fire by His grace and generosity. He is the best One to ask and the most noble to hope for.

I seek refuge in Allah from the accursed Satan: 'The month of Ramadan is that in which the Quran was revealed, as guidance for mankind and clear proofs of guidance and the criterion. So whoever among you witnesses the month, let him fast. And whoever is ill or on a journey, then a number of other days. Allah intends for you ease and does not intend for you hardship, so that you may complete the period and glorify Allah for having guided you, and perhaps you will be grateful. And when My servants ask you about Me — I am indeed near. I respond to the supplication of the supplicant when he calls upon Me. So let them respond to Me and believe in Me, that they may be guided.'

May Allah bless me and you through both revealed scriptures, and benefit me and you through the guidance of the master of all creation. I say this and seek forgiveness from Allah for me and for you and for all Muslims from every sin — so seek His forgiveness. He is indeed the Oft-Forgiving, the Most Merciful.

All praise is due to Allah who guided us to Islam as a favor and gift from Him, and made fasting an act of drawing close to Him, a shield, and a means leading to His pleasure and Paradise. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, the God of mankind and jinn. And I bear witness that our Prophet Muhammad is the servant of Allah and His Messenger, sent with the most complete law and the most upright Sunnah. May Allah send His peace, blessings, and salutations upon him, his family, and his companions — those of lofty resolve and tranquil souls — and upon those who followed them in excellence until the Day of Judgment.

To proceed: Fear Allah, O servants of Allah, and thank Him for reaching this great season — the season of trading with the Most Generous, the Most Forbearing.

He distinguished him with acts of worship unlike any other. The righteous predecessors, may Allah have mercy on them, followed that path — setting the finest examples in perfecting their fast, realizing its true meaning, and filling its nights with night prayer and righteous deeds. Al-Zuhri, may Allah have mercy on him, said: 'When Ramadan enters, it is only a month of reciting the Quran and feeding food.' And they would sit in the mosques saying: 'We guard our fast and do not backbite anyone.' Allahu Akbar! So fear Allah, O servants of Allah, in increasing righteous deeds in this noble month — especially the recitation of the Quran in the month of the Quran — while making the most of the honor of this time and place.

In the Hadith, Rasulullah صلى الله عليه وسلم said: 'Whoever reads a letter from the Book of Allah shall receive a good deed for it, and a good deed is multiplied ten times. I do not say that Alif-Lam-Mim is a letter, but Alif is a letter, Lam is a letter, and Mim is a letter.' This was reported by the compilers of the Sunan from the Hadith of Ibn Mas'ud رضي الله عنه.

Know, O brothers in Islam, that just as you welcomed this month, you will soon bid it farewell. You welcomed it with joy, humility, and submission to Allah, and you will soon bid it farewell with grief and tears. The first ten days have nearly passed, and many people have turned away from obedience to their Lord. Do you know, O servant of Allah, whether you will reach the rest of the month or not complete it? The wise and perceptive person is one who seizes that as an opportunity to take account of his soul, straighten its crookedness, and force it toward obedience to its Lord before death overtakes it suddenly — for at that point nothing will benefit it but righteous deeds.

Remember, may Allah watch over you, that you are welcoming the middle ten days of Ramadan — the ten of forgiveness — as the Chosen Prophet صلى الله عليه وسلم informed us. So take hold of the means of forgiveness through repentance, turning to Allah, and maintaining righteous deeds. 'And indeed I am Ever-Forgiving to whoever repents and believes and does righteous deeds and then remains guided.' May Allah make us and you among those for whom mercy, forgiveness, and freedom from the Fire have been written in this noble month. He is indeed Most Generous and Bountiful.

Know, O servants of Allah, that among the best of your deeds, the most elevating to your ranks, the purest before your King, and the heaviest on your scales is your frequent sending of prayers and peace upon the beloved, the chosen Prophet, the leader of those who fast and pray, the master of all creation, the Messenger of the Lord of the worlds — the best of those who prayed and fasted and the finest of those who stood before Allah in the night. Your Lord has commanded you to do so in the noblest of speech: 'Indeed, Allah and His angels send prayers upon the Prophet. O you who believe, send prayers upon him and salute him with worthy greetings of peace.'

O Allah, send Your peace, blessings, and salutations upon our master and our beloved. And be pleased, O Allah, with his rightly guided successors, his Companions, the Followers, and those who followed them in excellence until the Day of Judgment — and with us alongside them by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful.

O Allah, honor Islam and the Muslims, humiliate polytheism and the polytheists, destroy the enemies of the religion, and make this land safe, secure, prosperous, and comfortable — and all the lands of the Muslims.

O Allah, grant us security in our homelands, reform our Ummah and our leaders, and support with truth our Imam and the guardian of our affairs. O Allah, grant him success in what You love and are pleased with, take him by his forelock toward goodness and piety, and provide him with righteous advisors who direct him to good and help him toward it.

O Allah, grant all leaders of the Muslims the ability to rule by Your Sharia and to follow the Sunnah of Your Prophet Muhammad صلى الله عليه وسلم. O Allah, make them a mercy for those they govern, O Possessor of Majesty and Honor.

O Allah, grant victory to our mujahid brothers in Your path and those oppressed for their religion in every place. O Allah, grant them victory in Palestine, Kashmir, and Chechnya.

Our God — mighty is Your protection, majestic is Your praise, holy are Your names, there is no god but You, and there is no power or might except through You.

O Allah, You know what has befallen our Muslim brothers in Chechnya. O Allah, hasten their victory, O Most Powerful, O Most Mighty.

O Allah, forgive Muslim men and women, believing men and women, unite their hearts, guide them to the paths of peace, and keep them away from immorality and tribulations — both visible and hidden.

O Allah, repel from us inflation, plague, usury, fornication, earthquakes, and tribulations — both visible and hidden — from our land and all Muslim lands, O Lord of the worlds.

There is no god but You. You are the Self-Sufficient and we are the needy. Send down rain upon us and do not make us among those who despair. O Allah, relieve us. O Allah, relieve us. O Allah, relieve us. O Allah, bless our lands with goodness and rain, and our hearts with faith and certainty, O Lord of the worlds.

Our Lord, grant us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire. Our Lord, accept from us — indeed You are the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing — and relent toward us — indeed You are the Ever-Relenting, the Most Merciful. Forgive us and our parents and all Muslims, the living among them and the deceased, by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful.

Glory be to your Lord, the Lord of Honor, above what they attribute to Him, and peace be upon the messengers, and all praise is due to Allah, the Lord of the worlds.
