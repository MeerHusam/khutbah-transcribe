This Friday khutbah reminds us that water is a great blessing from Allah and that we must conserve it, avoid waste, and always be grateful.

DETAILED SUMMARY
================
This Friday khutbah focuses on water as one of Allah's greatest blessings, citing multiple Quranic verses that highlight how water gives life to the earth and everything in it. The khatib emphasizes that true gratitude for blessings is not just words but is expressed through preserving and using them wisely, reminding the congregation that Islam strictly prohibits wastefulness in all things, including water. Drawing on the Sunnah of the Prophet, peace and blessings of Allah be upon him, the khatib highlights that he used minimal amounts of water for wudu and bathing, and that even wasting water beside a flowing river is forbidden. The second part of the khutbah stresses that water conservation is a collective responsibility that falls on every individual, household, and institution, and specifically calls out the common habit of opening bottled water and leaving it unfinished. The khutbah closes with heartfelt supplications for the country, its leadership, its security forces, and all Muslims.

FULL TRANSLATION
================
All praise is due to Allah, the Bestower of blessings, the Most Generous, who sends down water from the clouds and has made it a source of life and mercy for lands and people. We praise Him, glorified be He, and thank Him for His abundant blessings and great gifts. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner, and I bear witness that our master, our Prophet, and our leader Muhammad is His servant and messenger. May Allah's peace, blessings, and salutations be upon him, his family, and all his companions. To proceed: O servants of Allah, I counsel you and myself to fear Allah, for it is the counsel of Allah to those who came before and to those who come after. May Allah make me and you among His righteous servants. Brothers in faith, Allah has blessed us with countless blessings that cannot be enumerated. He, the Exalted, has commanded us to be grateful for them, and has promised those who are grateful an increase, while warning those who deny and are ungrateful of punishment. Our topic today, may Allah have mercy on me and you, concerns one of those great blessings, a blessing whose need never ceases, and without which there is no life for humans, animals, or plants. For He, the Exalted, has said: Brothers in faith, water is one of the signs of Allah. It is a blessing that Allah has bestowed upon His servants: water He sends down from the sky, water He draws out from the earth, and water He causes to flow within it. There are salty waters in the seas and fresh waters, and in each of them there are profound wisdoms and great benefits. Among His signs is that He shows you lightning, bringing both fear and hope, and sends down water from the sky, reviving the earth after its death. Indeed, in that are signs for people who reason. And among His signs is that you see the earth still and barren, but when We send down water upon it, it stirs and grows. Indeed, He who gives it life is surely the giver of life to the dead. Indeed, He is over all things competent. And He, the Exalted, said: Do they not see that We drive water to barren land and bring forth crops from which their livestock and they themselves eat? Will they not then see? And He, the Exalted, said: O servants of Allah, always remember this great blessing. Let us praise Allah, the Exalted, and thank Him for making it plentiful and readily available. Let us look at our own condition and at the condition of those who obtain water only with great difficulty, and then receive only a small amount that may not even be fit for drinking. Let us thank Allah for these blessings, encourage one another to be grateful for them, and raise our children and those in our care to preserve and appreciate them. For gratitude for blessings is a means of preserving them and increasing them. Brothers in faith, water is not something trivial to be wasted, nor a cheap commodity to be squandered. Rather, it is a blessing to be thanked for, a trust to be preserved, and a wealth to be protected. O Muslims, gratitude for blessings is not merely a word to be said. It is not simply a word spoken by the tongue. Rather, it means protecting the blessing from being lost and using it well in what is beneficial. As He, the Exalted, said: "If you are grateful, I will surely increase you." Our great Shari'ah has brought a magnificent approach that governs all consumption in just a few comprehensive words. Allah, the Exalted, has made this a guiding principle: So there is no excess in food, drink, clothing, wealth, or water. Moderation is guidance and extravagance is misguidance. Good management is gratitude and wasteful squandering is ingratitude and destruction. The beloved Prophet, peace and blessings of Allah be upon him, was guided by moderation in all things. Part of his Sunnah regarding water was frugality. Anas, may Allah be pleased with him, reported: "The Prophet, peace and blessings of Allah be upon him, used to perform wudu with one mudd of water and take a bath with one sa' up to five mudds." Narrated by Al-Bukhari and Muslim. The Prophet, peace and blessings of Allah be upon him, passed by Sa'd, may Allah be pleased with him, while he was performing wudu, and said: "What is this extravagance, O Sa'd?" Sa'd said: "Is there extravagance even in wudu?" He said: "Yes, even if you are by a flowing river." Narrated by Imam Ibn Majah and Ahmad, and graded hasan by Al-Albani. So if extravagance is forbidden even when one is beside a flowing river, what then of one who turns on the tap and lets the water run without need, or chats with those beside him, and what of one who uses a great deal when a little would suffice? O servants of Allah, if conserving water is an obligation in our homes, then caring for it in the facilities of mosques and public places is an even greater trust. So preserve, may Allah have mercy on you, these blessings, and especially this great blessing of water. In particular, we find, and all praise is due to Allah, an abundance of cold and fresh water such that we are now able to choose from various water sources, deciding what water to drink and what water to buy. All praise is due to Allah, outwardly and inwardly. Do not let, O servants of Allah, the abundance of water be a cause for wasting it, and do not let its easy availability be a cause for forgetting its value. For blessings endure through gratitude, are preserved through good care, are wasted through extravagance, and are taken away through ingratitude. O Allah, perpetuate Your blessings upon us, bless us in what You have provided for us, help us to be grateful to You and to care well for Your blessings, and protect us from extravagance, waste, heedlessness, and falling short. I say what you have heard and I seek forgiveness from Allah, the Magnificent and Majestic, for myself, for you, for our parents, for our spouses, for our children, for our neighbors, for our relatives, and for all Muslims. So seek His forgiveness, All praise is due to Allah for His goodness, and gratitude to Him for His guidance and His favor. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner, in magnification and reverence of His majesty. And I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and messenger, who called to His pleasure. May Allah's peace, blessings, and salutations be upon him, his family, his companions, and his brothers, and upon all those who followed his path and walked in his footsteps. May Allah reward them by His grace and generosity, for He is the Most Generous of the generous. To proceed: My counsel to myself and to you is to fear Allah, the Exalted and the Most High. So fear Allah, that you may receive mercy. O servants of Allah, let us know that preserving water is not the responsibility of one party and not another, nor of one individual and not another. Rather, it is a trust carried by all and a responsibility for which each person will be asked according to his capacity. The Prophet, peace and blessings of Allah be upon him, said: "Each of you is a shepherd and each of you is responsible for his flock." Narrated by Imam Al-Bukhari and Muslim. So let each of us begin with himself, his household, and those under his care. There is to be no extravagance in wudu or in bathing, no waste in the home, no negligence in the mosque, and no squandering in public facilities. Water is a blessing, so be grateful for it. It is a trust, so preserve it. It is a wealth, so do not waste it and do not squander it. Know, may Allah have mercy on me and you, that conservation does not mean deprivation, and moderation does not mean going without. Rather, it means using what is needed and protecting against waste. There is no surplus to be squandered and no blessing to be scattered. O servants of Allah, let us reflect on our condition, and especially on what Allah has blessed us with in this country and elsewhere, namely the blessing of fresh and palatable water reaching us in our homes and in packaged form in bottles of all sizes. Let us thank Allah. Likewise, let us appreciate what the generous and charitable people do by distributing water in mosques and public places. We find that some among us, may Allah guide us all, take more water than they need, and some of it is left over. This is what we see in many occasions, gatherings, events, and wedding celebrations. You find some people opening a bottle and leaving half of it or more, thinking that this somehow shows refinement or something of the sort. By Allah, such a person will be asked about that water he left behind, since no one else will drink from it after him. It is therefore incumbent upon us, O servants of Allah, to advise one another and to be among the grateful servants of Allah. O Allah, preserve for our country its security, faith, prosperity, and stability. Bless us in its water, goodness, and provisions. Make us grateful for Your blessings, mindful of Your favors, and obedient and repentant to You. With this, send your peace and blessings, may Allah have mercy on me and you, upon the one whom we are commanded to honor with salutations. O Allah, send Your blessings upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, as You sent Your blessings upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim. Indeed, You are the Most Praiseworthy, the Most Glorious. And bless, O Allah, Muhammad and the family of Muhammad, as You blessed Ibrahim and the family of Ibrahim. Indeed, You are the Most Praiseworthy, the Most Glorious. O Allah, be pleased with the Rightly Guided Caliphs, the rightly directed leaders: Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman, and Ali, and with all the companions of Your Prophet, his wives the mothers of the believers, and those who follow them in goodness. Grant us their reward by Your grace, generosity, and bounty, O Most Generous of the generous. O Allah, O All-Mighty, strengthen Islam and the Muslims. O Allah, O Supporter, grant victory to Islam and the Muslims. O Allah, O Protector, protect Islam, the Muslims, and the lands of the Muslims. O Allah, O Best of protectors, protect our country, the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia: its security, prosperity, stability, goodness, sanctities, those in authority, its security and defense personnel, and all residents and citizens within it, O Best of protectors. O Allah, deal with the aggressing Houthis. O Allah, deal with all the enemies of Your religion. O Allah, deal with all those who intend evil or harm toward our country and the other lands of the Muslims. O Allah, Lord of all the worlds, turn their scheming into destruction and ruin upon themselves, O Strong, O Mighty. O Allah, grant success to our security and defense personnel. O Allah, make them aware of Your enemies and the enemies of our country. O Allah, reward them on our behalf with the best of rewards, O Possessor of Majesty and Honor. O Allah, count their fallen among the martyrs and heal their wounded, O Best of protectors, O Lord of all the worlds. O Allah, grant success to, protect, and guide our ruler, the Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques, King Salman bin Abdulaziz, and his trustworthy Crown Prince, Prince Muhammad bin Salman, and their supporters. O Allah, extend their deeds in Your obedience, and grant them, O Allah, health and well-being. O Allah, strengthen them, grant them victory, and do not let any plot succeed against them, O Possessor of Majesty and Honor. O Allah, forgive our scholars and teachers. O Allah, forgive our fathers and mothers and have mercy on them as they raised us when we were young. O Allah, heal and cure our sick and the sick among the Muslims. O Allah, heal and cure our sick and the sick among the Muslims, and relieve us and our Muslim brothers of debt. O Allah, Lord of all the worlds, O Allah, relieve us and our Muslim brothers of debt. And peace be upon the messengers, and all praise is due to Allah, Lord of all the worlds.
