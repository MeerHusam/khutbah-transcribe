الحمد لله المنعم الوهاب، منزل الماء من السحاب، جعله حياة ورحمة للبلاد والعباد، نحمده سبحانه ونشكره على جزيل نعمه وعظيم عطاياه، وأشهد أن لا إله إلا الله وحده لا شريك له، وأشهد أن سيدنا ونبينا وإمامنا محمداً عبده ورسوله، فصلى الله وسلم وبارك عليه وعلى آله وأصحابه أجمعين أما بعد: عباد الله، أوصيكم ونفسي بتقوى الله، فهي وصية الله للأولين

All praise is due to Allah, the Bestower of blessings, the Most Generous, who sends down water from the clouds and has made it a source of life and mercy for lands and people. We praise Him, glorified be He, and thank Him for His abundant blessings and great gifts. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner, and I bear witness that our master, our Prophet, and our leader Muhammad is His servant and messenger. May Allah's peace, blessings, and salutations be upon him, his family, and all his companions. Let us look at our own condition and at the condition of those who obtain water only with great difficulty, and then receive only a small amount that may not even be fit for drinking. Let us thank Allah for these blessings, encourage one another to be grateful for them, and raise our children and those in our care to preserve and appreciate them. For gratitude for blessings is a means of preserving them and increasing them.

Brothers in faith, water is not something trivial to be wasted, nor a cheap commodity to be squandered. Rather, it is a blessing to be thanked for, a trust to be preserved, and a wealth to be protected.

O Muslims, gratitude for blessings is not merely a word to be said. It is not simply a word spoken by the tongue. Rather, it means protecting the blessing from being lost and using it well in what is beneficial.

So there is no excess in food, drink, clothing, wealth, or water. Moderation is guidance and extravagance is misguidance. Good management is gratitude and wasteful squandering is ingratitude and destruction.

Do not let, O servants of Allah, the abundance of water be a cause for wasting it, and do not let its easy availability be a cause for forgetting its value. For blessings endure through gratitude, are preserved through good care, are wasted through extravagance, and are taken away through ingratitude. O servants of Allah, let us reflect on our condition, and especially on what Allah has blessed us with in this country and elsewhere, namely the blessing of fresh and palatable water reaching us in our homes and in packaged form in bottles of all sizes. Let us thank Allah. Likewise, let us appreciate what the generous and charitable people do by distributing water in mosques and public places. We find that some among us, may Allah guide us all, take more water than they need, and some of it is left over. This is what we see in many occasions, gatherings, events, and wedding celebrations. You find some people opening a bottle and leaving half of it or more,

اللهم وارض عن الخلفاء الراشدين الأئمة المهديين، أبي بكر وعمر وعثمان وعلي وعن سائر أصحاب نبيك وزوجاته أمهات المؤمنين والتابعين لهم بإحسان وعنا معهم بمنك وجودك وكرمك يا أكرم الأكرمين، اللهم يا عزيز أعز الإسلام والمسلمين، اللهم يا ناصر انصر الإسلام والمسلمين، اللهم يا حافظ احفظ الإسلام والمسلمين وبلاد المسلمين، اللهم يا خير الحافظين احفظ على بلادنا المملكة العربية السعودية

O Allah, be pleased with the Rightly Guided Caliphs, the rightly directed leaders: Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman, and Ali, and with all the companions of Your Prophet, his wives the mothers of the believers, and those who follow them in goodness. Grant us their reward by Your grace, generosity, and bounty, O Most Generous of the generous. O Allah, O All-Mighty, strengthen Islam and the Muslims. O Allah, O Supporter, grant victory to Islam and the Muslims. O Allah, O Protector, protect Islam, the Muslims, and the lands of the Muslims. O Allah, O Best of protectors, protect our country, the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia:

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

أمنها ورخاءها واستقرارها وخيراتها ومقدساتها وولاة أمرها ورجال أمنها ودفاعها والمقيمين والمواطنين فيها يا خير الحافظين، اللهم عليك بالحوثيين المعتدين، اللهم عليك بأعداء دينك أجمعين، اللهم عليك بمن أراد بلادنا وسائر بلاد المسلمين بسوء أو مكروه، اللهم يا رب العالمين اجعل تدبيره تدميراً وهلاكاً عليه يا قوي يا عزيز، اللهم وفق رجال أمننا ودفاعنا، اللهم بصرهم بعدوك وعدو بلادنا، اللهم

its security, prosperity, stability, goodness, sanctities, those in authority, its security and defense personnel, and all residents and citizens within it, O Best of protectors. O Allah, deal with the aggressing Houthis. O Allah, deal with all the enemies of Your religion. O Allah, deal with all those who intend evil or harm toward our country and the other lands of the Muslims. O Allah, Lord of all the worlds, turn their scheming into destruction and ruin upon themselves, O Strong, O Mighty. O Allah, grant success to our security and defense personnel. O Allah, make them aware of Your enemies and the enemies of our country. O Allah,

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

جازهم عنا خير الجزاء يا ذا الجلال والإكرام.

reward them on our behalf with the best of rewards, O Possessor of Majesty and Honor.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

اللهم تقبل موتاهم في الشهداء وداوي وداوي مرضاهم يا خير الحافظين يا رب العالمين، اللهم وفق واحفظ وسدد ولي أمرنا خادم الحرمين الشريفين الملك سلمان بن عبد العزيز وولي عهده الأمين الأمير محمد بن سلمان وأعوانهم، اللهم مد بأعمالهم على طاعتك، ومتعهم اللهم بالصحة والعافية، اللهم قوهم وانصرهم ولا تمكر عليهم يا ذا الجلال والإكرام.

O Allah, count their fallen among the martyrs and heal their wounded, O Best of protectors, O Lord of all the worlds. O Allah, grant success to, protect, and guide our ruler, the Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques, King Salman bin Abdulaziz, and his trustworthy Crown Prince, Prince Muhammad bin Salman, and their supporters. O Allah, extend their deeds in Your obedience, and grant them, O Allah, health and well-being. O Allah, strengthen them, grant them victory, and do not let any plot succeed against them, O Possessor of Majesty and Honor.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

اللهم اغفر لمشايخنا وعلمائنا، اللهم اغفر لآبائنا وأمهاتنا وارحمهم اللهم كما ربونا صغاراً، اللهم شافي وداوي مرضانا ومرضى المسلمين، اللهم شافي وداوي مرضانا ومرضى المسلمين واقض الدين عنا وعن إخواننا المسلمين. اللهم يا رب العالمين، اللهم اقض الدين عنا وعن إخواننا المسلمين،

O Allah, forgive our scholars and teachers. O Allah, forgive our fathers and mothers and have mercy on them as they raised us when we were young. O Allah, heal and cure our sick and the sick among the Muslims. O Allah, heal and cure our sick and the sick among the Muslims, and relieve us and our Muslim brothers of debt. O Allah, Lord of all the worlds, O Allah, relieve us and our Muslim brothers of debt.

📚 Hadith  ·  Narrator: Anas reported that Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) used to supplicate (in these words)  ·  Collection: Sahih Muslim
