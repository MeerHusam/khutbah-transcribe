ANNOTATED READER VIEW
=====================

الحمد لله الغفور الودود ذي الفضل والكرم والإحسان والجود. أحمده على نعمه الضافية البرود ومننه الصافية الورود.

All praise is due to Allah, the Most Forgiving, the Most Loving, the possessor of bounty, generosity, goodness, and grace. I praise Him for His abundant blessings, cool and refreshing, and His pure and beautiful gifts.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

وأشهد أن لا إله إلا الله وحده لا شريك له شهادة ترغم أنف الجحود.

I bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner — a testimony that humbles the nose of every denier.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

وأشهد أن نبينا وسيدنا محمداً عبده ورسوله أشرق نور رسالته في التهايم والنجود وبدد ظلام الجهل ولياليه السود.

And I bear witness that our Prophet and master Muhammad is His servant and Messenger, whose light of prophethood shone across the lowlands and the highlands, dispelling the darkness of ignorance and its long black nights.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

صلى وسلم عليه وعلى آله وأصحابه ما ناح قمري وأورق عود.

May Allah's peace and blessings be upon him, his family, and his companions, for as long as a turtledove coos and a branch puts forth leaves.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

أما بعد فيا أيها المسلمون اتقوا الله تقوى من تداوى بالطاعات واحتما عن المحرمات

To proceed: O Muslims, fear Allah as those who seek remedy through acts of obedience and shield themselves from what is forbidden.

O Muslims, Allah created Paradise and made it a home for His devoted friends, a dwelling for His chosen ones. He filled it with His mercy, honor, and pleasure. He encouraged people to desire it, called them toward it, and named it the Abode of Peace.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

دار لا ينفد نعيمها ولا يبيد. دار فيها من كل خير مزيد قد تشوقت لطالبيها وتزينت لمريديها ونطقت أدلة الكتاب والسنة بوصف ما فيها.

It is an abode whose bliss never runs out and never perishes. An abode in which every goodness is ever increasing. It has longed for those who seek it and adorned itself for those who desire it, and the proofs of the Quran and the Sunnah have spoken eloquently of what lies within it.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

فيا سعادة ساكنيها ويا فوز وارثيها. عن أبي هريرة رضي الله عنه عن رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم قال: قال الله تعالى: أعددت لعبادي الصالحين ما لا عين رأت ولا أذن سمعت ولا خطر على قلب بشر.

How blessed are its inhabitants, and how triumphant are those who inherit it. Narrated by Abu Hurayrah, may Allah be pleased with him, from the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, who said: Allah the Exalted said: I have prepared for My righteous servants what no eye has seen, no ear has heard, and what has never occurred to the heart of any human being.

He will say: I was commanded not to open it for anyone before you. Paradise has eight gates, and the distance between the two panels of each gate of Paradise is like the distance between Makkah and Hajar, or between Makkah and Busra.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

وأول زمرة يدخلون الجنة على صورة القمر ليلة البدر والذين يلونهم على أشد كوكب ذري في السماء إضاءة.

The first group to enter Paradise will appear like the full moon on the night of the full moon, and those who follow them will shine like the most brilliantly glowing star in the sky.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

لا يبولون ولا يتغوطون ولا يمتخطون ولا يتفلون. أمشاطهم الذهب ورشحهم المسك ومجامرهم الألوة.

They will not urinate, defecate, blow their noses, or spit. Their combs will be of gold, their perspiration will be musk, and their incense burners will be of aloe wood.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

لا اختلاف بينهم ولا تباغض على قلب رجل واحد على صورة أبيهم آدم ستون ذراعاً في السماء في عرض سبعة أذرع يسبحون الله بكرة وعشيا.

There will be no disagreement among them and no hatred. Their hearts will be as one heart, in the form of their father Adam, sixty cubits tall and seven cubits wide. They will glorify Allah morning and evening.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

يدخل أهل الجنة الجنة جرداً مرداً بيضاً جعاداً مكحلين أبناء ثلاث وثلاثين قد أشرق على وجوههم السناء والضياء والجمال والبهاء

The people of Paradise will enter it hairless, beardless, white-skinned, with curly hair, with kohl-lined eyes, at the age of thirty-three. Their faces will be radiant with light, brightness, beauty, and splendor.

From it spring the rivers of Paradise. The greatest, sweetest, and most beautiful of these rivers is the river of Al-Kawthar, which Allah made as an honor for our Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him.

Its banks are domes of hollowed pearl, its soil is fragrant musk, its pebbles are pearls, its water is whiter than milk and sweeter than honey, its vessels are as numerous as the stars, and birds with necks like those of camels drink from it. And the basin of the Prophet, peace be upon him, in the land of the gathering

is as wide as it is long, its distance from one end to the other being a month's journey, or like the distance between Sana'a and Madinah. Two channels pour into it from Paradise, and the people of faith will come to it.

Whoever drinks from it will never be thirsty again. And some people will surely be turned away from it — those who altered, changed, and introduced new things.

The lowest in rank among the people of Paradise is a man who comes after all the people of Paradise have already entered. He is told: Enter Paradise. He says: O my Lord, how, when people have already taken their places and received their shares?

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

أخذاتهم فيقال له أترضى أن يكون لك مثل ملك ملك من ملوك الدنيا فيقول رضيت رب فيقول لك ذلك ومثله ومثله ومثله ومثله فيقول في الخامسة رضيت رب فيقول هذا لك وعشرة أمثاله ولك ما اشتهت نفسك ولذت عينك فيقول رضيت رب.

He is asked: Are you pleased to have the equivalent of a kingdom belonging to one of the kings of this world? He says: I am pleased, my Lord. He is told: You have that, and the same again, and the same again, and the same again, and the same again. On the fifth time he says: I am pleased, my Lord. He is told: You have this and ten times its like, and you have whatever your soul desires and whatever delights your eyes. He says: I am pleased, my Lord.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

وأما أعلاهم منزلة فيقول الله فيهم أولئك الذين أردت غرست كرامتهم بيدي وختمت عليها فلم تر عين ولم تسمع أذن ولم يخطر على قلب بشر.

As for the highest in rank, Allah says of them: Those are the ones I willed. I planted their honor with My own hand and sealed it, so no eye has seen it, no ear has heard it, and it has never occurred to the heart of any human being.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

وإن في الجنة لشجرة يسير الراكب في ظلها مائة سنة ما يقطعها وإن للمؤمن في الجنة لخيمة من لؤلؤة واحدة مجوفة طولها في السماء ستون ميلاً للمؤمن فيها أهلون يطوف عليهم المؤمن فلا يرى بعضهم بعضا.

In Paradise there is a tree in whose shade a rider could travel for a hundred years without crossing it. And the believer in Paradise will have a tent made from a single hollowed pearl, sixty miles high. The believer will have wives within it and he will go around to them, and they will not be able to see one another.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

ولو أن امرأة من أهل الجنة اطلعت على أهل الأرض لأضاءت ما بينهما ولملأته ريحاً ولنصيفها على رأسها يعني خمارها خير من الدنيا وما فيها.

And if a woman from the people of Paradise were to look out upon the people of the earth, she would illuminate everything between them and fill it with fragrance. And the scarf upon her head, meaning her veil, is better than this world and all that is in it.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

وما في الجنة أعذب وإن الله عز وجل يقول لأهل الجنة يا أهل الجنة فيقولون لبيك ربنا وسعديك والخير في يديك

And what is in Paradise is sweeter still. Allah, the Mighty and Majestic, will say to the people of Paradise: O people of Paradise! They will say: Here we are, our Lord, at Your service, and all goodness is in Your hands.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

He will say: Are you pleased? They will say: Why would we not be pleased, O our Lord, when You have given us what You have given no one else among Your creation? He will say: Shall I not give you something even better than that? They will say: And what could be better than that? He will say: I bestow My pleasure upon you and I will never be angry with you after this, ever.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

فما أعظم هذا النعيم وما أجل هذا التكريم وإن العطاء الأعظم والنعيم الأكبر الذي يتضاءل أمامه كل نعيم هو النظر إلى وجه الله الكريم.

How magnificent is this bliss, and how sublime is this honor. The greatest gift and the supreme joy before which all other joys pale is the sight of the noble Face of Allah.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

عن جرير بن عبد الله رضي الله عنه قال: كنا عند رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم فنظر إلى القمر ليلة البدر وقال إنكم سترون ربكم عياناً كما ترون هذا القمر لا تضامون في رؤيته متفق عليه وما

Allah is the Greatest. He has blessed them and given, granted them their wishes and more. May Allah make me and you among the people of happiness, and grant us goodness and increase.

I say what you have heard, and I seek Allah's forgiveness for myself, for you, and for all Muslims from every sin and wrongdoing. So seek His forgiveness, for He is ever forgiving to those who constantly return to Him.

with a magnificent status in the hearts of Muslims. In it are Al-Masjid Al-Haram and Al-Masjid Al-Nabawi Al-Sharif and the sacred sites. In it is where revelation descended and where our Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him, migrated, and from it the message of Islam was launched.

O Allah, be pleased with the Rightly Guided Caliphs and the rightly guided Imams, those of manifest honor and elevated standing: Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman, and Ali, and with all the family and companions, and with us alongside them, O Most Generous, O Most Bestowing.

O Allah, grant strength to Islam and the Muslims, humiliate shirk and the polytheists, destroy the enemies of the religion, and protect our land — protect our land, the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia — and all the lands of the Muslims from the plots of the plotters, the schemes of the schemers, the malice of the spiteful, and the envy of the envious, O Lord of all the worlds.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

O Allah, grant our leader and guardian, the Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques, success in what You love and are pleased with, and guide him by his forelock toward righteousness and piety.

O Allah, grant him and his Crown Prince success in what brings honor to Islam and goodness to the Muslims, O Lord of all the worlds.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

اللهم احفظ جنودنا واحمي حدودنا واجمع شملنا واخز عدونا واهد أبناءنا وبناتنا واغفر لنا ولوالدينا وللمسلمين يا رب العالمين.

O Allah, protect our soldiers, guard our borders, unite us, humiliate our enemy, guide our sons and daughters, and forgive us, our parents, and all Muslims, O Lord of all the worlds.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

اللهم احفظ جنودنا المرابطين على حدودنا وثغورنا. اللهم اشف مرضاهم وداو جرحاهم وتقبل موتاهم في الشهداء وانصرهم على عدوك وعدوهم يا سميع الدعاء وانصرهم على عدوك وعدوهم يا سميع الدعاء.

O Allah, protect our soldiers stationed on our borders and frontier posts. O Allah, heal their sick, treat their wounded, accept their fallen as martyrs, and grant them victory over Your enemy and theirs, O You Who hears all supplications. Grant them victory over Your enemy and theirs, O You Who hears all supplications.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

اللهم اشف مرضانا وعاف مبتلانا وارحم موتانا يا رب العالمين.

O Allah, heal our sick, grant wellbeing to those who are afflicted, and have mercy on our deceased, O Lord of all the worlds.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

اللهم طهر المسجد الأقصى من دنس المعتدين. اللهم أغثنا، اللهم أغثنا، اللهم أغثنا، اللهم أنزل علينا الغيث، اللهم أنزل علينا الغيث، اللهم أنزل علينا الغيث ولا تجعلنا من القانطين.

O Allah, purify Al-Masjid Al-Aqsa from the defilement of the aggressors. O Allah, relieve us, O Allah, relieve us, O Allah, relieve us. O Allah, send down rain upon us, O Allah, send down rain upon us, O Allah, send down rain upon us, and do not make us among those who despair.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

اللهم اجعل دعاءنا مسموعاً ونداءنا مرفوعاً يا كريم يا عظيم يا رحيم.

O Allah, make our supplication heard and our call raised, O Most Generous, O Most Great, O Most Merciful.
