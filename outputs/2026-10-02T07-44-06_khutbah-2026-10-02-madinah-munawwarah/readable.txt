DETAILED SUMMARY
================
This Friday khutbah is devoted to a rich and detailed description of Paradise, drawing on Hadith to paint a vivid picture of its beauty, its rivers, its people, and its eternal bliss. The khatib describes everything from the construction of Paradise out of gold and silver bricks with musk as mortar, to the appearance of its inhabitants, the river of Al-Kawthar, the Basin of the Prophet, peace be upon him, and the immense tent of the believer. He emphasizes that the greatest reward of all will be beholding the Face of Allah, and that Allah's bestowal of His eternal pleasure upon the people of Paradise surpasses every other gift. In the second part, the khatib urges Muslims not to squander this immense prize for the sake of a fleeting and lowly world, reminding them that death is near and that the goods of Allah are precious. The khutbah closes with heartfelt supplications for the Ummah, for the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and its leadership, for soldiers on the frontlines, for the liberation of Al-Masjid Al-Aqsa, and for rain.

FULL TRANSLATION
================
All praise is due to Allah, the Most Forgiving, the Most Loving, the possessor of bounty, generosity, goodness, and grace. I praise Him for His abundant blessings, cool and refreshing, and His pure and beautiful gifts. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner — a testimony that humbles the nose of every denier. And I bear witness that our Prophet and master Muhammad is His servant and Messenger, whose light of prophethood shone across the lowlands and the highlands, dispelling the darkness of ignorance and its long black nights. May Allah's peace and blessings be upon him, his family, and his companions, for as long as a turtledove coos and a branch puts forth leaves. To proceed: O Muslims, fear Allah as those who seek remedy through acts of obedience and shield themselves from what is forbidden. O Muslims, Allah created Paradise and made it a home for His devoted friends, a dwelling for His chosen ones. He filled it with His mercy, honor, and pleasure. He encouraged people to desire it, called them toward it, and named it the Abode of Peace. It is an abode whose bliss never runs out and never perishes. An abode in which every goodness is ever increasing. It has longed for those who seek it and adorned itself for those who desire it, and the proofs of the Quran and the Sunnah have spoken eloquently of what lies within it. How blessed are its inhabitants, and how triumphant are those who inherit it. Narrated by Abu Hurayrah, may Allah be pleased with him, from the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, who said: Allah the Exalted said: I have prepared for My righteous servants what no eye has seen, no ear has heard, and what has never occurred to the heart of any human being. This is agreed upon. It is an abode whose radiance glows, whose courtyards are pleasant, and whose construction is magnificent. Its bricks are of gold and silver, its mortar is the finest fragrant musk, its pebbles are pearls and precious gems, and its soil is saffron. Whoever enters it will enjoy ease and never suffer hardship, will live forever and never die. His garments will never wear out and his youth will never fade. And the first to knock on the gate of Paradise will be our Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him. The gatekeeper will say to him, the gatekeeper will say to him: Who are you? He will say: Muhammad. He will say: I was commanded not to open it for anyone before you. Paradise has eight gates, and the distance between the two panels of each gate of Paradise is like the distance between Makkah and Hajar, or between Makkah and Busra. The first group to enter Paradise will appear like the full moon on the night of the full moon, and those who follow them will shine like the most brilliantly glowing star in the sky. They will not urinate, defecate, blow their noses, or spit. Their combs will be of gold, their perspiration will be musk, and their incense burners will be of aloe wood. There will be no disagreement among them and no hatred. Their hearts will be as one heart, in the form of their father Adam, sixty cubits tall and seven cubits wide. They will glorify Allah morning and evening. The people of Paradise will enter it hairless, beardless, white-skinned, with curly hair, with kohl-lined eyes, at the age of thirty-three. Their faces will be radiant with light, brightness, beauty, and splendor. A caller will announce to them: You shall live and never die, you shall be healthy and never fall ill, you shall be young and never grow old, and you shall enjoy ease and never suffer hardship, ever. And if a man from the people of Paradise were to look out and reveal his bracelet, it would outshine the light of the sun just as the sun outshines the light of the stars. When you ask Allah, ask Him for Al-Firdaws, for it is the middle of Paradise and the highest part of Paradise, and above it is the Throne of the Most Merciful. From it spring the rivers of Paradise. The greatest, sweetest, and most beautiful of these rivers is the river of Al-Kawthar, which Allah made as an honor for our Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him. Its banks are domes of hollowed pearl, its soil is fragrant musk, its pebbles are pearls, its water is whiter than milk and sweeter than honey, its vessels are as numerous as the stars, and birds with necks like those of camels drink from it. And the basin of the Prophet, peace be upon him, in the land of the gathering is as wide as it is long, its distance from one end to the other being a month's journey, or like the distance between Sana'a and Madinah. Two channels pour into it from Paradise, and the people of faith will come to it. Whoever drinks from it will never be thirsty again. And some people will surely be turned away from it — those who altered, changed, and introduced new things. The lowest in rank among the people of Paradise is a man who comes after all the people of Paradise have already entered. He is told: Enter Paradise. He says: O my Lord, how, when people have already taken their places and received their shares? He is asked: Are you pleased to have the equivalent of a kingdom belonging to one of the kings of this world? He says: I am pleased, my Lord. He is told: You have that, and the same again, and the same again, and the same again, and the same again. On the fifth time he says: I am pleased, my Lord. He is told: You have this and ten times its like, and you have whatever your soul desires and whatever delights your eyes. He says: I am pleased, my Lord. As for the highest in rank, Allah says of them: Those are the ones I willed. I planted their honor with My own hand and sealed it, so no eye has seen it, no ear has heard it, and it has never occurred to the heart of any human being. In Paradise there is a tree in whose shade a rider could travel for a hundred years without crossing it. And the believer in Paradise will have a tent made from a single hollowed pearl, sixty miles high. The believer will have wives within it and he will go around to them, and they will not be able to see one another. And if a woman from the people of Paradise were to look out upon the people of the earth, she would illuminate everything between them and fill it with fragrance. And the scarf upon her head, meaning her veil, is better than this world and all that is in it. And what is in Paradise is sweeter still. Allah, the Mighty and Majestic, will say to the people of Paradise: O people of Paradise! They will say: Here we are, our Lord, at Your service, and all goodness is in Your hands. He will say: Are you pleased? They will say: Why would we not be pleased, O our Lord, when You have given us what You have given no one else among Your creation? He will say: Shall I not give you something even better than that? They will say: And what could be better than that? He will say: I bestow My pleasure upon you and I will never be angry with you after this, ever. How magnificent is this bliss, and how sublime is this honor. The greatest gift and the supreme joy before which all other joys pale is the sight of the noble Face of Allah. Narrated by Jarir ibn Abdullah, may Allah be pleased with him: We were with the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, when he looked at the full moon and said: You will surely see your Lord clearly, just as you see this moon — you will have no difficulty in seeing Him. This is agreed upon. And nothing stands between the people and beholding their Lord except the veil of majesty upon His face, in the Garden of Eden. A caller will call out: O people of Paradise, Allah has a promise He wishes to fulfill for you. They will say: What is it? Have our scales not been made heavy, our faces not been made white, have we not been admitted into Paradise and saved from the Fire? The veil is then lifted and they behold Him, and nothing He has ever given them is more beloved to them than gazing upon Him. Allah is the Greatest. He has blessed them and given, granted them their wishes and more. May Allah make me and you among the people of happiness, and grant us goodness and increase. I say what you have heard, and I seek Allah's forgiveness for myself, for you, and for all Muslims from every sin and wrongdoing. So seek His forgiveness, for He is ever forgiving to those who constantly return to Him. All praise is due to Allah, a praise befitting His blessings. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner, and no true object of worship besides Him. And I bear witness that our Prophet and master Muhammad is His servant, Messenger, chosen one, and elect. May Allah's peace and blessings be upon him, his family, and his companions, continuously and perpetually until the Day of Judgment. O you who O Muslims, those were some of the descriptions of Paradise and its bliss. So how can a squanderer throw away such bliss for the sake of a lowly world that has announced its departure and hastened its end? Beware, beware, O servants of Allah. Death is bound to your forelocks, and this world is being folded away behind you. Many a wound has proven fatal, and many a stumble has led to ruin. Whoever fears sets out by night, and whoever sets out by night reaches the destination. Indeed, the merchandise of Allah is precious. Indeed, the merchandise of Allah is Paradise. O Muslims, let us recall with gratitude the grace of Allah and His abundant gift and kindness in what He has blessed this blessed land with: the blessing of security and faith, unity of ranks, and agreement of word. A land that Allah has honored with serving the Two Holy Mosques and has distinguished with a magnificent status in the hearts of Muslims. In it are Al-Masjid Al-Haram and Al-Masjid Al-Nabawi Al-Sharif and the sacred sites. In it is where revelation descended and where our Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him, migrated, and from it the message of Islam was launched. This land has always been and continues to be a home of sound belief, a beacon of knowledge, an arena of service to Muslims, and to those heading to the Two Holy Mosques. Send your peace and blessings upon Ahmad, the guide, the intercessor for all mankind. Whoever sends one blessing upon him, Allah sends ten blessings upon that person in return. O Allah, bestow peace and blessings upon our Prophet and master Muhammad, the herald of mercy and reward, the warner of might and punishment, the intercessor whose intercession is accepted on the Day of Reckoning. O Allah, be pleased with the Rightly Guided Caliphs and the rightly guided Imams, those of manifest honor and elevated standing: Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman, and Ali, and with all the family and companions, and with us alongside them, O Most Generous, O Most Bestowing. O Allah, grant strength to Islam and the Muslims, humiliate shirk and the polytheists, destroy the enemies of the religion, and protect our land — protect our land, the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia — and all the lands of the Muslims from the plots of the plotters, the schemes of the schemers, the malice of the spiteful, and the envy of the envious, O Lord of all the worlds. O Allah, grant our leader and guardian, the Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques, success in what You love and are pleased with, and guide him by his forelock toward righteousness and piety. O Allah, grant him and his Crown Prince success in what brings honor to Islam and goodness to the Muslims, O Lord of all the worlds. O Allah, protect our soldiers, guard our borders, unite us, humiliate our enemy, guide our sons and daughters, and forgive us, our parents, and all Muslims, O Lord of all the worlds. O Allah, protect our soldiers stationed on our borders and frontier posts. O Allah, heal their sick, treat their wounded, accept their fallen as martyrs, and grant them victory over Your enemy and theirs, O You Who hears all supplications. Grant them victory over Your enemy and theirs, O You Who hears all supplications. O Allah, heal our sick, grant wellbeing to those who are afflicted, and have mercy on our deceased, O Lord of all the worlds. O Allah, purify Al-Masjid Al-Aqsa from the defilement of the aggressors. O Allah, relieve us, O Allah, relieve us, O Allah, relieve us. O Allah, send down rain upon us, O Allah, send down rain upon us, O Allah, send down rain upon us, and do not make us among those who despair. O Allah, make our supplication heard and our call raised, O Most Generous, O Most Great, O Most Merciful.
